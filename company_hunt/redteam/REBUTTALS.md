# Rebuttals and downgrades (main agent, 2026-11-21)

I read each dossier next to its red-team file, which was written without the red team seeing the bull case.

**AMP: red team WOUNDED. Kept, downgraded to a small position.** The red team's scariest fact is that operating cash flow ($8.3B) runs at 2.3x net income because of derivative collateral, which makes the screen's 18% FCF yield a mirage. That is correct and it matters. The dossier, though, never relied on that number: it values Ameriprise on about $4.0B of owner cash, roughly what the company actually returned in the last four quarters ($3.94B), and on 10-11x adjusted operating earnings of $4.16B. The blows that land are the 39% fall in Retirement & Protection earnings in Q3 2026 and earnings sitting near a cyclical high. Together they cut confidence to 6/10 and argue for a small position. Neither kills the thesis.

**MBGL: WOUNDED. Kept as a watch-list name, not a buy.** The red team's points are a 41% fall in net income in the first standalone quarter, SG&A up 50%, and $2.0B of new notes. The dossier saw all three and concluded the price sits inside fair value, not below it. Agreed: buy below $19-20 only.

**AYI: WOUNDED. Agreed, watch only.** The $51.3M of one-time tariff refunds (about 7% of operating profit) erases the core lighting margin gain. That reinforces the dossier's "fairly priced" verdict.

**TXT: WOUNDED. Agreed, watch only.** Bell "continued working on the program at its own risk" after MV-75 funds ran out, and the catch-up charge rose to $110-150M. Combined with the dossier's finding that core P/E is about 18x, not 12.5x, there is no margin of safety.

**GGG: WOUNDED. Agreed, priced in.** Gross margin fell about 3 points, mostly organically, and cash dropped from $624M to $140M as acquisitions and buybacks were funded with the revolver. The dossier reached the same conclusion independently.

**SW: WOUNDED, near DEAD. Downgraded to avoid.** Free cash flow of $240M against $712M of dividends, the gap funded with $334M of new debt, a 4.1% ROIC and an unsized DOJ criminal containerboard probe. I do not defend it.

Pattern across the red-team files: three of the six survivors (GGG, AYI, MBGL) added meaningful debt in late 2026. The kill test's balance-sheet gate was run on Q3 2026 balance sheets and missed borrowings made after quarter-end. Rule added for any future run: re-run gate 3 on 8-Ks filed after the latest 10-Q.
