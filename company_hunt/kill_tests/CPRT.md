## Reading
Copart runs the largest US salvage-car auction network; FY26 revenue was flat at $4.67B and profit fell 4.4% because fewer cars arrive at auction even as revenue per car rises, and the stock is down 51% from its high at about 20x earnings. The business is high quality (35% operating margin, no real debt, $4.5B of cash and Treasuries) and the stock is lightly debated, but two things blocked a pass. First, there is a three-year-old DOJ anti-money-laundering investigation into how Copart polices its auction buyers, which the company itself says it cannot size; second, management just committed $1.9B (42% of its liquid funds) to buy ACV Auctions, an unrelated dealer-wholesale platform, right after the largest buyback in its history at higher prices than today. Volume is the open question and I could not verify a multi-year US unit trend. Verdict: reject until the DOJ matter closes; it is the best-quality name in this batch and one to revisit.
