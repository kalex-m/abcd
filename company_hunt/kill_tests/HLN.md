## Reading
Haleon is the former GSK consumer-health business (Sensodyne, Advil, Voltaren, Centrum) with a 22.9% adjusted operating margin, GBP 1.9bn of free cash flow and net debt of 2.6x EBITDA. It is cheap against P&G and Church & Dwight because organic growth fell from 8.0% to 5.0% to 3.0% over 2023-2025, then (unverified from filings) to about 2.5% in 2026, mostly from price, while the share-gained-or-held KPI dropped from 71% to 58% of categories. The financial gates are fine: no customer above 10%, no Zantac exposure, diluted shares down 2.6% in two years, FCF above earnings. It fails on attention. The slowdown is the single most discussed fact about this stock, the 15% one-day fall followed a third consecutive miss against management's own 4-6% target, and there is no overlooked fact left to exploit; I also could not read the Q3 statement itself, which makes any claim that the miss is temporary a guess. Takeaway: a solid staples portfolio priced for the slow growth it is actually delivering, so rejected on gate 10.
