## Reading
Futu is a Hong Kong-based retail broker that went from $0.14B to $2.94B of revenue in six years, with a 61.6% operating margin and 4.0M funded accounts growing 28%, trading at about 7-8x earnings. The price reflects a regulatory event the filings confirm: the China securities regulator ordered it to stop serving mainland clients within two years and proposed a roughly US$271M fine, and Q3 2026 revenue growth already slowed to 4.5% with client assets falling 4.6%. Its operating cash flow and "debt" mostly track customer money, so the screen's cash numbers do not mean what they say and I could not verify regulatory capital. Eighteen analysts and a 31% one-day fall mean the bear case is fully noticed. Takeaway: reject on regulator concentration, the regulatory cap, and attention.
