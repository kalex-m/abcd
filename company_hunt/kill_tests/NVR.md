## Reading
NVR is the most capital-efficient US homebuilder: it does not own land, it buys finished lots on options, and it returns almost every dollar by repurchasing stock (shares down 21.6% in five years). Q3 2026 earnings fell 20% per share as orders fell 7%, prices 5% and gross margin 2.8 points to 18.3%, with lot costs and price concessions doing the damage, and the stock sits 43% off its high. The gates that matter come out as follows: the cash cushion was spent on buybacks near the top ($1.2B this year, down to $1.09B against $0.91B of notes), $1.03B of lot deposits are now partly impaired, insiders sold $208M and bought almost nothing, and the stock is not cheap on forward earnings (about 16-17x), while the housing downturn is the most discussed bear case in the market. Verdict: reject; a great franchise at a price that already reflects a weak cycle without a visible edge over those who follow it.
