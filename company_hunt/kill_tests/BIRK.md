## Reading
Birkenstock is a premium sandal and shoe brand growing 15% in constant currency with a 59% gross margin and 34% adjusted EBITDA margin, priced at about 13x forward earnings after a 43% fall on US tariffs, a slowing growth path and a sponsor that sold $1.1B of stock. The business numbers are real and the leverage is moderate (about 2.2x including the tax receivable liability). It fails on two gates. Its own 20-F says internal control over financial reporting was ineffective at 2025-09-30 because of IT and entity-wide material weaknesses carried over from the prior year, and no remediation is documented before the December 2026 report. And it fails on attention: 22 analysts, 12.9% short, a visible block sale and constant tariff coverage mean the bear case is known. Takeaway: rejected on gates 6 and 10; revisit only after the 2026-12-10 results and the FY2026 20-F show the material weaknesses are cleared.
