## Reading
Carpenter melts specialty alloys for jet engines and defence parts and has used scarce capacity to push the Specialty Alloys margin to 37.7%. The Phase 3 line that the drop is one quarter of planned maintenance downtime is true as far as the release goes: operating income was $196.4M, up 28% y/y but down 5% sequentially, and Q2 is guided to $192-197M. What Phase 3 understated is the size of the fall: the stock is down 56% from its July high, not 20%, with a 20.6% one-day drop on the print. The balance sheet is sound (net debt about $0.6B), and no customer is above 10% of sales, but cash flow turned slightly negative in the quarter, insiders sold $121M and bought nothing, and management bought back stock around $350 and authorised $1B more near the top. The valuation is only roughly in line with history, so the market has studied the issue and not clearly mispriced it. Takeaway: the best quality business in this batch and a close call, but the nobody-cares gate fails and the pay design and capital-allocation timing are unverified.
