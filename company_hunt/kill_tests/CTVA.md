## Reading
CTVA is the crop-protection half of old Corteva since the seed business left as Vylor on 1 October 2026; it fell 24% on 12 November when its first standalone guide slashed FY26 EBITDA to $1.10-1.125B because Brazil sales collapsed (-31% in Q3) under channel inventory, farmer credit stress and generic copies of ageing molecules, with price down 7%. The 50% FCF yield in the screen belongs to the combined company and means nothing here. The real business has 17.6% pro forma EBITDA margin on about $5.4B of nine-month sales and real new-product momentum, but the pricing erosion is the moat question and it is happening now, the cash cushion nets to roughly zero after pension and the PFAS share, there is no standalone cash history, and the drop was the most-covered event in the stock's life. Insider buying at $9.30 is the only clean positive. Verdict: reject; revisit if Brazil stabilises and a clean standalone balance sheet and a year of cash flow exist.
