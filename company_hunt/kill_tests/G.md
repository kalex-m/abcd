## Reading
Genpact runs finance, procurement, insurance and banking processes for large clients and is selling a growing agentic-AI and data business that grew 30% in Q3 2026. Revenue was up 7.3%, gross margin reached 38.0%, and the balance sheet is fine at about 0.9x EBITDA after netting. What the kill test cannot clear is cash and controls: nine-month operating cash flow fell to $142M from $526M because the ERP rollout stalled invoicing, a cash-flow classification error from 2025 was just corrected, and the 71% core business is flat, so relevance depends on AI substitution that nobody can prove. Three unverified gates add up to a fail on the rules; the stock is cheap (9.3x forward earnings) but the bear case is the best-known one in the sector.
