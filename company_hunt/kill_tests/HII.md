## Reading
Huntington Ingalls builds the Navy's aircraft carriers and a large share of its submarines, and its backlog of $86.2B (verified in the 10-Q) is nearly seven years of revenue. The stock is down because the yards turn that backlog into profit slowly: revenue is up 77% over nine years while operating income is down, with 46% of work on fixed-price incentive contracts that absorb labour and supply cost overruns. The Q3 2026 margin recovery to 7.0% and $429M of free cash flow is encouraging but partly payment timing. The balance sheet has $0.4B of cash against about $3.5B of net debt and retiree-type liabilities, and the issue the market is discounting is exactly the one it has debated for years, with 12 analysts covering it. Takeaway: a real franchise with a poor profit record and no cushion; it fails on balance sheet, management record and "nobody cares".
