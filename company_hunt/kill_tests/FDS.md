## Reading
FactSet sells financial data and analytics workflow tools to banks, asset managers and wealth advisers. FY2026 showed users up 9.8%, organic ASV up 7%, retention above 95% by value, and a 1.8-point adjusted margin slip from AI and platform spending; free cash flow was $0.71B, $0.64B went to buybacks, and leverage is a modest 1.3x. The business is sound, but it fails the "nobody cares" test: 12.6% of the float is short, 16 analysts follow it and the AI-disruption bear case is the central story, so the discount reflects a debated view rather than an overlooked one. Insiders sold $72M and bought $0.3M, and an antitrust class action on its CUSIP asset is unquantified. Takeaway: good business, fairly watched, no clear mispricing.
