## Reading
Owens Corning is the second-largest US roofing, insulation and doors maker; the roofing and insulation franchises still earn 21-29% EBITDA margins, but the group fell to a 3.6% operating margin and a $0.5B net loss in 2025 as the Masonite doors purchase turned into $1.6B of impairments (the entire Doors goodwill is now written off), roofing volumes fell with a second year of weak storms, and Q3 2026 sales dropped 7%. It carries $4.7B of net debt (about $5.3B with leases and benefits) against an $8.8B market cap and 1.4x interest cover, two customers make up 28% of sales, and management spent $0.8B on buybacks near the top. The franchise is real, but the balance sheet and the capital allocation record both fail the gates. Verdict: reject; a good roofing company inside an over-levered, mismanaged conglomerate.
