## Reading
Acuity makes commercial and industrial lighting plus a growing building-controls and audio business. Lighting is flat (-1% in FY2026) while controls grow mid-teens at 20%+ margins; FY2026 sales rose 6.8% to $4.6B, adjusted EPS 10.5% to $19.90, operating cash flow was $826M and the company bought back $287M of stock. The balance sheet is clean (net debt $0.06B), nothing is concentrated, accounting is clean and the pay plan rewards ROIC over cost of capital. The weaknesses are that executives sell regularly, the stock is only 13% off its high and sits exactly at its 10y median multiples, so the market is not wrong about anything here, it is simply fairly pricing a good, cyclical business with a 7.7% free cash flow yield. Takeaway: survives the kill test as a quality compounder, but the mispricing is thin.
