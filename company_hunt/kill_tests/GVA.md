## Reading
Granite builds public infrastructure and owns the quarries that supply it. The nine-month GAAP loss of $316.7M is mostly a non-cash and one-off cost of buying back the 3.75% convertible notes in 2026: $274M of discount amortisation and $255M of derivative loss in the cash flow statement, against an operating income that rose 54% to $320M. The Phase 3 reading that GAAP is hiding a good operating business is right on the operating line. But the company has net debt of about $1B, another in-the-money convertible, and a 2023 restatement (Item 4.02) over deferred-tax errors with a material weakness whose remediation I could not confirm. Earnings also lean on $44M of favourable contract-estimate releases. Takeaway: the least-followed name in the batch (6 analysts) and the operating story is real, but the restatement history and leverage fail it on gates 3 and 6.
