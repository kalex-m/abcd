## Reading
Deckers owns two brands, HOKA and UGG, that grew sales 9.8% to $5.47B in FY26 at a 23% operating margin with no debt and about a 10% FCF yield, and it is shrinking its share count about 9% a year. It is cheap because HOKA growth dropped from the high teens to 6.8% last quarter, domestic sales are flat, DTC comps are negative and tariffs are cutting gross margin. The financial gates pass cleanly: net cash after leases is $0.67B, cash conversion is about 1.0x, and nothing is concentrated. It fails on attention: the slowdown is the single most-discussed fact about the stock (9.3% short, serial downgrades), and insiders have sold $106M while buying almost nothing, so there is no information edge, and the brand moat is the kind that can fade without a visible warning. Verdict: a good, honest business at 10x earnings, but this is the market pricing a thoroughly studied slowdown, not missing it.
