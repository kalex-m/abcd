## Reading
FIS runs core processing for banks and, since January 2026, the card-issuer back end bought from Global Payments for $13.5B. The business is stable (recurring revenue +2.5%, 42.9% EBITDA margin, $24.5B RPO) but the equity is a thin layer on $20B of net debt, nearly $5.6B of it due or rolling within a year, after two guidance cuts and the CFO's exit. Its management record includes $18B of impairments, adjusted EPS of $5.75 against GAAP $0.73, and a purchase at peak leverage. The CEO and a director bought stock, which counts for something, but this is exactly what the market has studied: 23 analysts and a very visible bear case. Takeaway: too much debt and too little trust to survive, however low the multiple.
