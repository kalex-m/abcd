## Reading
Abbott makes glucose sensors, heart devices, diagnostics and nutrition products: $44.3B of 2025 revenue, 18% operating margin, comparable sales growth of about 7%. The stock is 23% below its three-year high because of three guidance disappointments in a year, $0.20 of dilution from the $21B Exact Sciences deal, slower electrophysiology growth against stronger rivals and an announced separation of the nutrition business. The business gates are good: relevance and moat hold, cash conversion is 1.15x, shares are flat, nothing is concentrated, and management insiders bought $4.5M including the CEO. The balance sheet is where the cushion went: $27B of net debt after Exact, ROIC down to 8%. It fails on legal exposure. The infant-formula litigation has already cost a $670M charge this quarter and is not finished, and a DOJ criminal investigation plus a False Claims Act suit (DOJ intervened) from the Sturgis plant remain open with no stated cap. Those are the kind of unbounded items that can reset the stock regardless of how well the devices do. Verdict: a high-quality healthcare franchise with a real discount to its own history, removed because the legal tail cannot be sized from the filings.
