## Reading
Ameriprise is a 10,000-advisor wealth manager with an annuity and asset-management arm; Q3 2026 adjusted operating EPS rose 22% to $12.07, wrap assets are $729B, and it returns nearly all profit (about $3.5B a year) to shareholders, cutting the share count 22% in five years. It is cheap on the screen only because bank and insurance cash flows look like 18% free cash flow, which they are not; on honest earnings it is about 12x, in line with its own history. The gates pass: capital at the bank and insurance sub is above requirements, adjusted earnings equal GAAP earnings, pay is tied to ROE, EPS and TSR, and nobody is arguing about it. The caution is that insiders have only sold, asset management is bleeding $11B a quarter, and $3.3B of unrealized bond losses are a paper risk if rates rise. Verdict: survives as a high-quality compounder, but it is not a mispriced one; it is a fair-price holding, not a discovery.
