## Reading
Graco makes spray, pump and dispensing equipment with a 28% operating margin and a 52% gross margin, and its stock sits 24% below its 3-year high after a Q3 2026 miss where gross margin fell about 3 points on a loss-making adhesives acquisition (Valco Melton), higher product costs and mix, and Americas volume stayed at zero growth. The franchise is intact and the cash gates are clean: OCF 1.16x earnings, flat share count, no concentration, minimal legal exposure, and almost no one is arguing about it (7 analysts). What the gates do not support is the Phase 3 balance-sheet ground: net cash is about zero after spending $463M on buybacks this year, and after leases and retirement liabilities it is slightly negative. It is also not statistically cheap (P/E 22 vs 28 median, 5.6% FCF yield). Verdict: survives as a quality industrial with a normal cycle problem, but the mispricing is thin: it is a fair price for a good business, not a bargain.
