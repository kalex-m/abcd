## Reading
Cummins makes engines for heavy trucks and pick-ups, and increasingly generators for data centres: Power Systems generation sales rose 29% for nine months to $2.13B, and Q3 EBITDA hit a record $1.8B, which I read in the 10-Q. The stock is 41% off its high on a Q3 revenue miss from supply constraints and weak pick-up demand. The balance sheet is acceptable at about $3.4B of net obligations against roughly $7B of annual EBITDA, and the customer base is healthy with PACCAR at 13%. The problems are management and attention: a $2.0B emissions settlement in 2023 on about one million pick-up engines, expensive Accelera write-offs, and no buybacks for three years, while the data-centre theme is already the best-known bull point on Wall Street and the stock still trades above its own historical multiple. Takeaway: a good industrial that is not neglected and has a poor compliance and capital-allocation record; rejected on gates 5 and 10.
