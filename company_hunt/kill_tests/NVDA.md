## Reading
NVIDIA is still growing 103% a year at a 75% gross margin, and I checked the CEO news myself: the 8-K Item 5.02 filed 2026-11-12 confirms Jensen Huang resigned immediately as CEO and director with no stated reason and CFO Colette Kress taking both jobs on an interim basis. The stock fell 4.3% that day, not 13%, and has drifted down 22% since 2026-11-06. The cheapness (about 14x annualised Q3 EPS) is the market pricing a peak in AI spending, plus a balance sheet that has grown a very large off-income-statement tail: $279B of supply commitments, $105B of capped guarantees and $25B of equity investments, while receivables have doubled and net income carries $25B of mark-to-market gains. With 55 analysts and $32B of daily volume, this is the most scrutinised stock there is, so the mispricing case that nobody is looking does not hold. Takeaway: an excellent business that fails on management continuity, concentration, export-control exposure and attention (gates 3, 5, 7, 8, 10); Form 4 insider pull did not return in time and is not used.
