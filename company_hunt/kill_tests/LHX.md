## Reading
L3Harris supplies solid rocket motors, sensors, radios and space payloads, and the Q3 release shows demand at record levels: orders of $19.3B, backlog of $53.2B, operating margin 13.0%. The stock is lower because its CEO was removed on 2026-08-16 over a Code of Conduct matter, the missile-unit IPO is delayed behind a $1B Department of War preferred investment, and defence stocks pulled back. Checked in the 10-Q, the balance sheet is more stretched than the card shows: $11.0B of debt (not $9.2B), $1.8B due in a year, net debt about $8B and nearly $9.4B once the government preferred and its warrants are counted. Twenty-one analysts follow it and every one of those events was a headline. Takeaway: good orders, a new CEO, a levered balance sheet and no neglect; rejected on gates 3, 5 and 10.
