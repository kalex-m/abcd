## Reading
Amrize is the largest North American cement and aggregates producer plus a roofing business, spun out of Holcim on 2025-06-23: $11.8B of revenue in 2025, 16% operating margin, $1.4B of FCF, a 2026 guide cut to $2.95-3.05B of EBITDA on oil-driven cost inflation and a 410bp roofing margin squeeze, and net debt of $5.5B (1.8x EBITDA). The Phase 3 reading holds on the facts: cement and aggregates prices are rising, buybacks are running ($750M this year), the share count is down about 2%, pay rewards EPS and cash conversion, and insiders are buying heavily, with CEO Jenisch alone at $64.7M and the group at $70.2M with no sales. What Phase 3 missed is the accounting: the 10-K reported a material weakness in U.S. GAAP expertise that is still open in the Q3 10-Q, with disclosure controls ruled not effective at 2026-09-30, on top of an auditor switch within the EY network. That is a hard fail under this test until remediated, and the valuation case is thin anyway because it already trades at 9.0x against CRH at 9.8x. Takeaway: a good materials franchise with an owner-minded CEO, held back by an unremediated control weakness and a price that is only a turn below its peers.
