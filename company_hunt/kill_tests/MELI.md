## Reading
MercadoLibre is growing 45% with payments volume up 53%, and the stock is down 31% in a year because operating margin fell from about 10% to 6.3% as it cut shipping fees, built warehouses and lent aggressively, with a loan book of $18B growing 62%. The screen's 14.9% free cash flow yield is not real: operating cash flow counts customer deposits that fund the loans, and loan growth is booked in investing. After netting there is no excess cash, the loan loss reserve is 27% of gross loans, and the credit regulators in Brazil and Argentina are a live variable. Twenty-six analysts follow it and the margin debate is the main story. Takeaway: a superb growth business whose cheapness comes from a cash-flow illusion and a known margin trade-off, not an oversight.
