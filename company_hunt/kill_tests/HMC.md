## Reading
Honda is the largest motorcycle maker in the world plus a captive lender and a car company that is now earning about nothing: H1 FY3/27 operating profit was JPY649bn, of which motorcycles JPY439bn and finance JPY211bn, and cars lost JPY5.7bn on JPY7.5tn of revenue. The Phase 3 case was that the market prices a cash-burning EV carmaker and ignores the two profit engines. The gates show the engines are real and the industrial balance sheet carries net cash of roughly JPY3.9tn after a first operating loss in decades, but the "unseen" label does not hold: the motorcycle-plus-finance valuation is the common bull case, segment profit is published every half-year, a major bank downgraded in March, Honda answered with a JPY1.1tn buyback that cut 18% of its shares in two years, and the stock is already 16% higher in a year and 7% below its 3-year high. I also could not verify pay design or insider behaviour for a Japanese filer. Takeaway: a good motorcycle business inside a car company that is already being valued on its parts, so there is no overlooked fact left to buy.
