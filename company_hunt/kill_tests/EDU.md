## Reading
New Oriental is China's biggest test-prep and tutoring company; after the 2021 ban on K-9 tutoring cut revenue by 27%, it rebuilt to $5.66B (FY2026), with Q1 FY27 revenue up 23.1% and operating margin 20.0%. The business is growing and profitable and returns cash, but the stock's discount is for something the filing shows directly: no equity ownership of the entities that earn the money, a regulator that has already erased most of the revenue once, a cash pile smaller than the tuition it has collected in advance, and directors selling. China education is the best-studied bear case in the US market with 21 analysts. Takeaway: reject on regulatory structure and attention; the growth is real but the risk is exactly what the price reflects.
