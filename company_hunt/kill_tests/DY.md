## Reading
Dycom builds fiber, wireless and data-centre connections for the telecom carriers, with record backlog of $12.24B that I confirmed in the August release. The stock fell from $400 to $294 within days of that release because about $150M of wireless revenue moved from FY2027 to FY2028 and Communications margin slipped 134 bps, and it kept sliding to $193 in early November. Customer concentration is as Phase 3 said: AT&T 20.3% and Verizon 11.5% of revenue for the six months, with AT&T at 25.4% for the full prior year. On top of that the company holds $2.5B of net debt, free cash flow turned slightly negative in the first half as receivables ballooned, and there is no pricing-power evidence. Takeaway: record demand, thin margins and a levered, customer-concentrated balance sheet; rejected on gates 2, 3, 7 and 10, with three further gates unverified.
