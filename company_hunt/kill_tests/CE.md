## Reading
Celanese bought DuPont's Mobility & Materials unit for about $11B with borrowed money in 2022, then watched autos, industrial demand and Chinese acetyl capacity turn against it, producing $5.8B of impairments, two years of GAAP losses and a stock down 71% from its high. Operations are recovering: Q3 2026 operating EBITDA $548M vs $517M and a raised $750-800M free cash flow target. The problem is that the equity is a $5.3B sliver on $10.2B of net debt with $1.4B due within a year, management's own 4.7x leverage target, and a free cash flow figure partly propped by receivable factoring that has just lost its European leg. The management record (acquire at the top, buy back at $100-160) is poor even though insiders are now buying, and the stock is not neglected: 18 analysts, 8.8% short, +33% in a year. This is a leveraged cyclical bet, not a market oversight.
