## Reading
JD is a Chinese retailer and logistics operator with USD 187B of revenue, a flat JD Retail profit of RMB15.1bn a quarter, listed stakes in JD Logistics, JD Health and JD Industrials, and a stock 69% below its five-year high after a self-chosen food-delivery fight cut group operating income from $5.3B to $0.4B. The Phase 3 case was a $54B SOTP against a $33.7B market cap. The gates take most of that away: the headline cash of RMB215bn is mostly supplier float, genuine excess cash is about RMB5-16bn after the Europe purchase, Q3 free cash flow was negative RMB23.9bn, electronics revenue is shrinking 7.5%, the regulator sets how hard platforms may compete, and management is still spending abroad rather than returning cash or separating the stakes. It also fails on attention: 35 analysts, a forward P/E of 6 and the delivery-war bear case are in every China internet note. Takeaway: a profitable core at a low price, but the debate is crowded, the cash is not a cushion, and management's last large decision lost the money the price now needs back.
