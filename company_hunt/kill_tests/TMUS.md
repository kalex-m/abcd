## Reading
T-Mobile is the fastest-growing of the three US carriers with 35.0M postpaid accounts, EPS up 34% and $5.1B of adjusted free cash flow in a quarter. The Phase 3 reading that the stock fell on slower account growth and higher churn is correct and I confirmed the numbers: nine-month net account adds are down 16%, and churn is 1.09% against 0.97% a year ago. The debt side is the heavy part: $83.8B of net debt and $112B including operating leases, with $9.6B of buybacks in nine months, some funded by new borrowing. The shares have sold off with 25 analysts watching and the bear case is the standard one in telecom, so the market is not looking away from anything. Insiders have bought only $3.9M in three years, all above today's price, while Deutsche Telekom sold $5.5B as part of its stake programme. Takeaway: a strong operator with a leveraged balance sheet and a fully discussed problem; rejected on gates 3 and 10.
