## Reading
Adobe still grows 13% with $27.5B of ARR and a 36.6% operating margin, and the damage from AI shows only in the stock-photo unit, in price increases being delayed and in a stock that fell 64% from its high while the multiple dropped to about 12x earnings. The financials are strong: $9.9B of free cash flow, near-neutral balance sheet, no customer concentration. What fails is attention and capital allocation. Thirty-three analysts and a daily AI-disruption debate mean the market is not overlooking anything, and management spent $11.3B on buybacks in FY2025 at around $370 a share before losing both its CEO and CFO in 2026. Takeaway: a great business at a debated price; the kill test fails it on "nobody cares" and the buyback record, not on quality.
