## Reading
NetEase is a profitable game studio (2025 revenue $16.1B, operating margin 31.8%, FCF $7.1B) trading at about 11x forward earnings with net cash equal to a third of its market value; Q3 2026 revenue slipped 1.8% as the 2025 hits matured and Youdao fell 12%, and that stall is all the market is reacting to. The business, the cash and the shareholder returns pass every operating gate. What fails is the one thing nobody can analyse away: the revenue flows through VIE contracts that no court has tested, game approvals can be halted by decree, and a delisting rule exists. That risk is permanent, which means the cheapness is a price for something real rather than a mistake, and I could not verify insider ownership or guidance. Verdict: reject on the regulatory structure; it is a good business at a fair China price, not a clear mispricing.
