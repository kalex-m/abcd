## Reading
Intuit sells QuickBooks, payroll, payments, Credit Karma and TurboTax; FY26 revenue rose 14% to $21.45B and GAAP EPS 20% to $16.46, with FY27 GAAP EPS guided up 22-24%, yet the stock has halved on the fear that AI agents will do taxes and bookkeeping for free, and TurboTax units are already falling (-2% to 39.0M). The cash flow is large ($8.6B), but $2.06B of it is paid in stock, the share count is up 1.5% in five years despite $5.4B of buybacks at far higher prices, the balance sheet is net debt of $1.6B after a $1.5B quarter of repurchases, an FTC final order and an IRS free-filing threat sit directly on the consumer franchise, and the AI-disruption debate is the most crowded bear case in software. Insiders, led by the founder and CEO, sold $1.35B. Verdict: strong QuickBooks franchise, but it fails on balance sheet, dilution, legal and attention, and the key question (whether AI substitutes the product) cannot be verified from filings.
