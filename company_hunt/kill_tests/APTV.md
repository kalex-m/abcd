## Reading
Aptiv is the post-spin connector, high-voltage and ADAS-electronics supplier left after Versigent was separated on 2026-04-01; half of the apparent 49% price collapse is bookkeeping and the real loss is about 32%. At 6.8x adjusted earnings it looks cheap, but the business has no war chest (net debt $4.7B), depends on ten customers for 56% of sales, shows falling margins, makes adjusted earnings 40% above GAAP with recurring restructuring, and its management spent $4.1B on buybacks in 2024 at prices more than double today's while a $648M goodwill write-off followed the Wind River deal. Cash conversion is genuinely good and dilution is negative, but the market has analysed the auto-share-loss story in detail. Takeaway: a decent supplier at a fair price for its risks, not a mispricing.
