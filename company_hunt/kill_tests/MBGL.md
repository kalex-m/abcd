## Reading
Mobility Global is CARFAX plus a B2B data unit for automakers and dealers, spun out of S&P Global on 2026-07-01: $1.75B of revenue in 2025, growing 7% with a 41% adjusted EBITDA margin and about $0.46B of FCF a year, now carrying $1.98B of 5-6% notes issued to pay S&P Global a $2.0B dividend. It trades at about 11x EBITDA against 13-20x for the data peers, a 6.5% FCF yield, and the stock is up 14% since the spin while 12 analysts follow it. The gates all pass: the moat is exclusive and hard-to-replicate data plus the CARFAX consumer brand, cash conversion is real (the GAAP profit is depressed by $296M a year of acquired-intangible amortization), no customer is above 10%, there is no pending material litigation, and one director bought $0.98M after the spin. What I could not verify is how management is paid or how it allocates capital, because the company is five months old; net debt of $1.6B at 2.1x EBITDA means there is no war chest, and the Tax Matters Agreement limits how fast it can buy back stock. Takeaway: a good data franchise at a modest discount to peers, with neither a clear mispricing nor a hidden flaw.
