## Reading
Cirrus Logic sells audio and power chips, and in FY26 91% of its $2.0B of sales went to one end customer, Apple, up from 87% two years ago. The business is excellent on the numbers (23% operating margin, 26% ROIC, $0.9B net cash, share count down 21% in ten years) and the stock is flat on a year after a spring 2026 spike to $178.90 and back to $116.80 as flat Q2 revenue and falling general-market sales reminded everyone that iPhone content is the whole story. That is not a mispricing; it is a priced-in single-customer dependency whose share keeps rising, with only three analysts watching and no insider buying. Verdict: reject on concentration; a quality chip designer whose fate is decided by one phone maker.
