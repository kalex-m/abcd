## Reading
Booking runs the world's largest online hotel marketplace, and it is growing: Q3 2026 room nights +7%, gross bookings +10%, adjusted EPS +17%, with a 47% adjusted EBITDA margin. The stock is down a third because investors fear AI assistants will intermediate travel search, and because the FTC extracted a $1.0B charge in Q3. Both are real, but the gates are decisive: the company is a net borrower ($20.1B of debt vs $15.8B of cash, and $9.9B of nine-month buybacks funded partly by new debt), the legal hits are aimed at parity clauses and pricing practices that protect its moat in Europe and the US, and 34 analysts have already debated every angle. Insiders have sold $178M and bought nothing. Verdict: excellent business, no balance-sheet safety, and no hidden angle; reject.
