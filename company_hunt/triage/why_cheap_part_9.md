## HMC (Honda Motor Co., Ltd.): UNSEEN, advance Y

Honda's headline is the EV disaster: JPY1.58tn of EV charges and a first annual operating loss in decades in FY3/26, and a FY3/27 guide of JPY780bn that still includes heavy EV losses. The 6-K for the six months to Sep 2026 shows what sits under it: motorcycles earned JPY439bn (+19%), financial services JPY211bn, and the automobile segment an operating loss of JPY5.7bn on JPY7.5tn of revenue. So the reported total is real money from two arms the narrative ignores, and the car arm is the one the market is discussing. It is not a trapped-asset case in the sense of a lock-up: both arms are wholly owned and consolidated. The risk is that EV losses and supplier compensation consume the cash, which the filing does not rule out. The market looks at the cars, not the segments, so this is unseen rather than judged. Verdict: UNSEEN, advance.

## BIDU (Baidu, Inc.): DOWN_FOR_REASON, advance N

Baidu is not ignored: 31 analysts cover it and the stock is down 57% from its three-year high because the market has read the same numbers I did. Online marketing revenue fell 22% in Q3 2026 to RMB11.9bn and is now under half of the general business, share is moving to ByteDance and Alibaba, and the company is pouring cash into GPUs (FY2025 free cash flow -$2.2bn) after a roughly $16bn impairment earlier in the year. The RMB276.6bn cash and investments pile is verified and the first dividend is promised before year end, but the SOTP treats that cash as distributable while management is clearly choosing to spend it. AI Cloud is growing fast (+94%) yet it replaces a decaying profit pool rather than adding to it. Verdict: down for a reason, reject.

## JD (JD.com, Inc.): HURT, advance Y

JD's weak price is a story the market can see: the food-delivery subsidy war with Meituan and Alibaba took the group to its first quarterly loss in nearly four years (Q4 2025) and halved annual profit, and the stock is 69% below its five-year high. What the Q3 2026 release shows is that the war is cooling: marketing spend fell 26%, New Businesses losses narrowed 'significantly', group operating income was RMB5.2bn and JD Retail held RMB15.1bn, flat on last year at a 5.8% margin. So the cheapness is a cost problem, not demand loss, and the core earns about $8bn a year. What stops me calling it a clear bargain is that the retail core is not growing, management is still spending abroad (CECONOMY), and part of the SOTP cushion is cash that sits inside listed subsidiaries and payables. Verdict: hurt, not broken; advance with the trap noted.
