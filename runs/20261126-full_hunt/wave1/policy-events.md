## Lead findings

1. **New US tariff proposal on 14 economies (Section 301, "structural excess capacity")**
   - Fact: On 2026-11-25 USTR found actionable practices in 14 economies and proposed extra tariffs: China up to 20%, EU 15%, Japan/Korea/Taiwan/Switzerland up to 15%, India up to 25%, Vietnam up to 35%, others up to 18-20%. No decision on Mexico or Norway. No effective date is stated. Written comments due 2027-01-05, hearings 2027-01-25.
   - Source: https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural (as of 2026-11-25)
   - Means: A proposal, not yet law, but it names sectors: autos, semiconductors, pharma, steel, solar, apparel, batteries, machine tools, rail equipment, robotics. Importers face higher input costs if finalized; it also keeps the tariff-inflation channel in the prior view alive into 2027. Confidence: high on the facts, medium on timing.
2. **Brazil Section 301 action: proposed add-ons** (2026-11-25): extra sugar tariff and a 10% tariff on finished Brazilian aircraft (ends an exemption). Requests to appear due 2026-12-07, comments 2026-12-30, hearing 2027-01-14. A 25% tariff on certain Brazilian goods has applied since the 2026-07-20 final action. Source: https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-invites-public-comment-proposed-modifications-section-301-action-regarding-brazils-unreasonable. Means: hurts Brazilian aircraft imports and sugar users. Confidence: high.
