## 1. The business in two sentences
Remitly runs a digital-only app that lets immigrants send money home, earning a fee plus an FX spread on each transfer. It is taking share from agent-based incumbents (Western Union, Intermex, Euronet's Ria) in corridors that themselves grow only ~2%.

## 3. What the price assumes
Reverse DCF: **~9.9% a year** owner-FCF growth for 10 years on the normalised $180M. That is roughly half of what management guides for 2027 (high teens) and of the 2026 pace (~22% revenue).

## 4. The mistake (revised after the deep dive)
The market prices Remitly as a fragile remittance trade that the new US remittance tax and immigration enforcement will break.
- **Holds:** the tax (26 USC 4475, 1%, transfers after 2025-12-31) applies only to cash, money order and cashier's-check funded sends; bank-account and US card funded sends are exempt by statute (`dd-regulatory.md`). Growth is share gain: +25% volume vs ~2% corridor inflows (`dd-peers.md`). The 2028 EBITDA target is nearly met two years early; 8 of 8 guided quarters beat (`dd-ir.md`).
- **Weaker than triage said:** cash flow is flattered by near-zero cash taxes; dilution exceeds buybacks (shares +8% in two years); the tax gave a one-off H1 2026 acquisition boost that makes 2027 comparisons harder; take rate is slipping (~6bp); revenue beats are shrinking (+7.3% to +0.8%); management turnover across CEO, accounting, product and legal chiefs in 2026.

## 5. What would make the market notice
- Q4 2026 results and 2027 guidance, around mid-February 2027 (date not confirmed): growth in the high teens with adjusted EBITDA margin holding near 22%.
- Buyback execution under the $500M authorisation (pace has slowed to ~$15M a quarter, `dd-credit.md`).
- Prosus fully exited by 2026-06-02, so the largest supply overhang is gone (`dd-ownership.md`).
