## 1. The business in two sentences
Intuit sells small-business software and money services (QuickBooks, payroll, payments: "Global Business Solutions", GBS) and consumer finance software (TurboTax, Credit Karma), mostly as subscriptions and per-return or per-transaction fees. GBS is the larger profit pool and still grows double digits; the Consumer side is shrinking in units.

## 3. What the price assumes
Reverse DCF: **~3% a year** owner-FCF growth for 10 years on $5.0B. The FY2027 guides imply more: GBS +13–14%, Credit Karma +3–7%, EPS guidance reiterated (`dd-diff.md`, `dd-transcripts.md`).

## 4. The mistake (revised after the deep dive)
The market prices all of Intuit as a business AI will hollow out.
- **Holds:** GBS keeps growing (FY26 +18% as recast; Q1 FY27 +15% like-for-like; Business Money payment volume +33%; mid-market ecosystem +41%) (`dd-diff.md`, `dd-transcripts.md`). GBS segment margin (~77% before shared costs) is far above peers' 7–25% operating margins (`dd-peers.md`). Government and FTC threats to TurboTax have faded: IRS Direct File is suspended with $0 in the FY27 budget line, the FTC "free" ad order was vacated on 2026-03-20 and the remaining suit dismissed on 2026-09-15 (`dd-regulatory.md`).
- **Weaker than the promotion assumed:** the Consumer side **is** losing relevance in units. US TurboTax units fell from 45.9M (FY21) to 39.0M (FY26) (`dd-ir.md`); IRS self-prepared e-files rose 1.7% in 2026 while TurboTax units fell (`dd-peers.md`); management now says it "lost quality DIY customers to lower-cost providers" and Credit Karma is "below expectations" with AI discovery named (`dd-transcripts.md`). The GBS long-term growth target was cut from 15–20% to 10–15% in August–September 2026 (`dd-diff.md`). GBS's "56–61% of profit" is measured before $7.3–9.4B of shared costs, so true GBS profit is not disclosed (`dd-edgar.md`, `dd-peers.md`).
What remains a mistake: a ~3% growth assumption for a company whose majority profit pool is guided to grow 10–15% and whose EPS has beaten the top of its guide nine quarters running.

## 5. What would make the market notice
