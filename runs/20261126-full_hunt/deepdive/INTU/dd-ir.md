# INTU dd-ir: company IR layer (as of 2026-11-26; IR pages fetched 2026-11-28)

## Summary for main thread
1. The triage said unit and customer counts are not disclosed. They are: the Sept 17 2026 Investor Day gave them. TurboTax US units fell from 45.9M (FY21) to 39.0M (FY26), IRS e-file share is 24.9% (-1.0 pt), and total online paid customers are 7.9M (+4%, below the old 5-10% target). Consumer platform customers are 37.4M (+2%).
2. Credit Karma FY27 guide of +11-13% was reiterated on Sept 17 and cut to +3-7% on Nov 19, 63 days later. Operating income guidance was held by lower stock pay, not revenue. GBS guide (+13-14%) is below the old 15-20% target; the new target is 10-15%.
3. The non-GAAP gap is smaller than the triage said: from FY27, stock pay is no longer excluded; the FY26 gap falls from $3.05B to $0.995B.

Thesis impact: weakens (GBS delivery holds, but its growth is slowing and Consumer relevance loss is now quantified)

## Findings

**F1. Disclosed unit and customer counts (Consumer).** US TurboTax units 45.9M, 43.3M, 41.4M, 40.7M, 39.9M, 39.0M for FY21-FY26 (-15% in five years; -2% in FY26, "concentrated in price-sensitive DIY"). TurboTax IRS e-file share 24.9%, -1.0 pt; DIY e-file share -3 pts; TurboTax Live customers 12.8M (+38%); Consumer platform customers 37.4M (+2%), ARPC $198 (+11%); lowest-spend bands shrank (-34% under $20, -11% $20-120). Source: [S1] pp.10, 107, 118-119, 158 (2026-09-17). Means: growth is price and mix on a flat-to-shrinking customer base; the lost customers are the cheap end, and the free Credit Karma Tax product ($0 federal, any complexity [S6]) targets that end. Confidence: high.

**F2. Disclosed customer counts (Business).** Total online paid customers 7.9M (+4%; 6.8M in FY23), US QuickBooks Online core paid 5.3M (+4%), US QBO retention 83%, mid-market 449K (+28%), ARPC $1.1K (+18%). Management: "new customer growth was not at the pace we expected"; customer growth slowed 6 pts since FY23. Source: [S1] pp.73-74, 158. Means: the 2024 and 2025 decks targeted 5-10% customer growth and 10-20% ARPC growth; FY26 customers missed the range, ARPC hit the top. GBS growth leans on price and mix, which is the part AI agents or switching could test first. Confidence: high.

**F3. GBS target vs delivery.** Long-term revenue target 15-20% (2023, 2024, 2025 decks); delivered +18% in FY25 and FY26 (ex-Mailchimp, recast). New target (2026): 10-15% a year for three years. FY27 guide +13-14%; implied Q2-Q4 growth about +12-13% (my arithmetic: guide $13,068-13,158M less Q1 $3,083M, over FY26 Q2-Q4 $8,918M). Online Ecosystem grew 25%, 25%, 22%, 20% across FY26 quarters, 20% in Q1 FY27, and Q2 is guided mid-to-high teens. Sources: [S2] p.125, [S3] p.125, [S4] p.127, [S1] pp.99, 157, [S5], [S7]. Means: "GBS growing 15%" is the current exit rate, falling toward the middle of a lowered range. Segment margin 76-77% (GBS operating income $8,928M, 56% of $15,840M segment total) is before about $10B of unallocated costs (GAAP operating income was $5,884M). Confidence: high.

**F4. Consumer target cut.** 2025 deck: TurboTax 6-10%, Credit Karma 10-15%. 2026 deck: Consumer 4-8% a year for three years, "near term customer growth should outpace ARPC growth". FY26 delivered TurboTax +7%, Credit Karma +20%. Sources: [S4] p.127, [S1] p.128. Means: management reset Consumer targets down before the Credit Karma cut. Confidence: high.

**F5. Guidance hit rate (company-level, from IR fact sheets and releases).**
- FY26 total revenue: initial $20,997-21,186M (Aug 2025), unchanged Q1 and Q2, raised to $21,341-21,374M (Q3), actual $21,448M. Credit Karma: $2,423-2,475M initial, actual $2,641M. TurboTax: $5,305-5,330M initial, $5,277-5,282M at Q3, actual $5,296M. GAAP operating income: $5,782-5,859M initial, $5,705-5,725M at Q3, actual $5,884M.
- Q1 FY27: guided revenue $4,294-4,313M, GAAP operating income $716-729M; actual $4,354M and $868M.
- FY27 Credit Karma: $2,919-2,973M set Aug 25 and reiterated Sept 17, cut to $2,721-2,826M on Nov 19 (midpoint -$172.5M, -5.9%). Total and Consumer revenue guides fell by the same amount.
- Mailchimp: Sept 2025 deck promised "double-digit Mailchimp revenue growth by FY26 exit"; Q4 FY26 growth was 0% and Q1 FY27 -1%.
Sources: [S8] (2025-08-21), [S9] (2026-05-20), [S10] (2026-08-25), [S5], [S11] (2026-09-17), [S12] (2025 deck p.64). Means: FY26 was beaten every quarter; the first miss is Credit Karma, in a segment where the company said at Investor Day it had the picture. Hit rate: strong in FY26, broken on the one line that matters for relevance. Confidence: high.

**F6. FY27 operating income held by lower stock pay.** CFO: operating income guidance reiterated "as cost savings, primarily from lower-than-expected share based compensation expense, will offset the impact from lower Credit Karma revenue". Q1 stock pay was $495M vs $521M guided; FY27 stock pay guide $2,020M (8.7% of revenue); FY30 target 8%. Sources: [S13] (2026-11-19), [S5], [S10]. Means: some of the profit protection comes from a lower stock price and the 17% headcount cut (May 2026, restructuring $293M actual vs $300-340M estimated [S14]), not from revenue. Confidence: medium (split of causes not disclosed).

**F7. Non-GAAP reconciliation size.** FY26 GAAP operating income $5,884M; adjustments: amortization of acquired tech $174M plus other acquired intangibles $485M, restructuring $293M, deferred-pay items $43M = $995M, giving $6,879M on the new basis. On the old basis (stock pay $2,056M excluded) non-GAAP was $8,935M, a $3,051M gap (52% of GAAP). Q1 FY27: adjustments $178M (20% of GAAP $868M). Stock pay is 9.6% of FY26 revenue. Sources: [S7] Table B2, [S1]/idfs26 [S15], [S10]. Means: the triage's "$3B gap" is now $1B; the new definition is more honest. Remaining adjustments are mostly Credit Karma and Mailchimp acquisition amortization, which falls as Mailchimp leaves. Confidence: high.

**F8. Mailchimp sale and buyback as presented.** Price about $3.68B: $3.31B cash plus $368M Bending Spoons stock. Close early calendar 2027, subject to regulatory approvals. Cash is to be used to repurchase shares within FY27 after close. FY26 Mailchimp revenue $1,272M, segment operating income $604M (47.5% margin); sale is about 2.9x revenue and 6.1x FY26 segment operating income. Remaining buyback authorization $6.4B; Q1 repurchases $1.5B. Guidance excludes the sale. Sources: [S7], [S13]. My arithmetic: $3.31B at $271.72 retires about 12.2M shares (4.6% of 267M); losing $604M segment income (24% tax) against that gives about 3% lower non-GAAP EPS before stranded-cost savings. Means: the sale is a way to shrink the weakest-growth unit, not an earnings boost; stock leg is unverifiable private paper. Confidence: medium.

**F9. Management's own AI evidence.** AI-agent discovery: TurboTax ranked first "about four times higher than the next tax brand"; QuickBooks AI connector over 2M calls a week, invoicing TPV run rate over $1B. Credit Karma "below expectations" with a "faster-than-anticipated" shift to AI-powered discovery. Source: [S13]. Means: the Consumer relevance risk is conceded by the company for Credit Karma, claimed solved for TurboTax and QuickBooks; no independent data here. Confidence: medium.

**F10. Q2 FY27 guide.** Consumer revenue down mid-single digits (about $1.40-1.43B vs $1,487M, my arithmetic), TurboTax down, about $30M of refund-transfer revenue shifts from Q2 to Q3; GBS low-to-mid teens. Q2 non-GAAP EPS $3.17-3.22. Source: [S13], [S5]. Means: the Feb 2027 print is guided to look weak on Consumer; the real test is Q3 (tax season). Confidence: high.
