# INTU dd-regulatory (run 20261126-full_hunt, as of 2026-11-28)

## Summary for main thread
1. Government free filing is not a live threat today. IRS Direct File is suspended and closed for the 2026 season, Treasury's own report (Oct 2, 2025) says to strengthen Free File rather than rebuild Direct File, and the FY2027 Treasury budget line for the task force is $0. No 2027 return plan was found.
2. The FTC "free" advertising order was vacated (5th Cir., Mar 20, 2026) and the remaining federal suit was dismissed Sep 15, 2026. The 2022 $141M state AG settlement is paid and old. No new state AG action found.
3. The legal note changed in one way that matters: two new securities class actions (Jul 10 and Aug 17, 2026) and two derivative suits (Sep 2026), plus Ontario class certification (Jul 24, 2026, Intuit appealing). Mailchimp sale needs HSR clearance; outside date Aug 19, 2027.

Thesis impact: neutral (regulatory risk to the Consumer side has fallen; litigation cost is unquantified but not thesis-breaking).

## Findings

**1. Direct File is suspended, with no 2027 plan found.**
- Fact: IRS told states Direct File "will not be available in Filing Season 2026" with no future launch date; the Direct File site reads "Direct File is closed." 296,531 returns were filed in 2025. Source: https://federalnewsnetwork.com/it-modernization/2025/11/irs-direct-file-will-not-be-available-in-2026-agency-tells-states/ (as of 2025-11, secondary report of the IRS email; the irs.gov page I tried returned 404).
- Fact: Treasury's "Report on the Replacement of Direct File" (Oct 2, 2025) says the IRS will suspend the tool and focus on strengthening Free File, and that Direct File had the lowest participation of the free options. Source: https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Report-Replacement-of-Direct-File-2025.pdf (2025-10-02). I could not extract the PDF text myself; this is from a search summary. Confidence on exact wording: medium.
- Fact: TIGTA report dated 2026-03-19 says the Direct File tool is no longer available. Source: https://www.tigta.gov/sites/default/files/reports/2026-03/2026408006fr.pdf (2026-03-19; via search summary).
- Fact: Treasury FY2027 budget justification shows the Direct File Task Force at $0 for FY2027, down from $15,000 thousand. Source: https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/266/03.-DOSE-FY-2027-BIB.pdf (FY2027; via search summary).
- Means: the government free option that could have pulled simple filers away from TurboTax is not funded. The "Direct File" risk in Intuit's 10-K now reads as "was suspended" (10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm, 2026-07-31), and the legacy Free File program could see wider adoption. A future administration could revive it, but nothing found says so. Confidence: medium-high.

**2. The FTC order is gone; the case is closed.**
- Fact: Fifth Circuit vacated the FTC cease-and-desist order and remanded on Mar 20, 2026 (in-house adjudication of deceptive-ad claims violates Article III under Jarkesy). Source: https://www.ca5.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/24/24-60040-CV0.pdf (2026-03-20).
- Fact: Intuit moved to dismiss the remaining FTC federal lawsuit as moot on Aug 31, 2026; it was dismissed without prejudice Sep 15, 2026. Source: Q1 FY27 10-Q Note 11, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000045/intu-20261031.htm (2026-11-19).
- Means: the feared "free" advertising order, which would have limited TurboTax's free-edition marketing, is not in force. "Without prejudice" means the FTC could in theory refile in federal court. I found no refiling, rehearing petition or cert petition (search only; the 5th Cir. docket was not checked). Confidence: high on vacatur and dismissal; medium that nothing new is pending.

**3. State attorneys general: nothing new.**
- Fact: 50 states and D.C. settled May 4, 2022 for $141M, paid by the quarter ended Jan 31, 2023. Source: 10-Q Note 11 (above), plus https://ag.ny.gov/index.php/press-release/2022/attorney-general-james-secures-141-million-millions-americans-deceived-turbotax (2022-05-04).
- Fact: a 22-AG amicus brief in 2024 supported the FTC order; it was a brief, not a suit. Source: https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/news/story/attorney-general-raoul-leads-coalition-urging-the-court-to-uphold-ftcs-ban-on-deceptive-turbotax-advertising (2024).
- Means: the 2022 settlement carried advertising commitments that still bind Intuit; the FTC vacatur does not erase them. No 2026 state action found in search; AG press-release sites were not searched one by one. Confidence: medium.

**4. Credit Karma: old FTC orders only; no new 2026 action found.**
- Fact: FTC finalized an order requiring Credit Karma to pay $3M and stop deceptive "pre-approved" claims (Sep 2022). Source: https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/FINAL%20CK%20Order%209-1-22.pdf (2022-09-01).
- Fact: the FY26 10-K says use of Credit Karma user data is subject to an FTC order requiring a security program and biennial independent assessments through 2034. Source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm (2026-07-31; via text read of risk factors).
- Fact: a private suit, Hladik v. Credit Karma, shows a notice of settlement dated 2026-02-20. Source: https://www.pacermonitor.com/public/case/61252270/Hladik_v_Credit_Karma,_LLC (2026-02-20; substance not seen).
- Means: compliance cost and a 2034 audit tail, but nothing that changes Credit Karma economics. The Credit Karma problem in the triage is demand (AI discovery, rates), not regulation. CFPB/FCRA-specific rules: nothing_found for a new 2026 rule touching Credit Karma. Confidence: medium.

**5. Mailchimp sale: antitrust clearance needed but low-overlap.**
- Fact: purchase agreement dated 2026-11-19 with Bending Spoons US Inc. Base price $3.68B, adjustable; Intuit reinvests about $368M for 8,818,595 Bending Spoons shares at closing. Closing conditions: HSR waiting period expiry or termination plus certain other specified regulatory approvals; no "Burdensome Condition"; no Intuit shareholder vote; no financing condition. Outside date Aug 19, 2027, extendable 3 months for regulatory approvals. Source: 8-K Item 1.01, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000119312526468834/d182669d8k.htm (2026-11-19). Break fees: not stated in the 8-K; Exhibit 2.01 not read.
- Means: I found no reporting on an HSR filing, second request or other review. Bending Spoons is a software roll-up with no obvious email-marketing overlap that I could confirm, so a block looks unlikely, but that is not evidenced. Risk is timing: the $3.3B buyback is tied to close. Confidence: medium.

**6. Legal-proceedings note year over year (Q1 FY26 10-Q vs Q1 FY27 10-Q).**
- Fact, Nov 2025 note: FTC order "pending" at the Fifth Circuit; state AG settlement; Ontario class action filed Aug 25, 2022 (no detail). Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687825000048/intu-20251031.htm (2025-11-20).
- Fact, Nov 2026 note: FTC order vacated and suit dismissed (Mar 20 and Sep 15, 2026). New: Ontario class certified Jul 24, 2026, Intuit filed notice of appeal; two shareholder class actions under Exchange Act 10(b)/20(a) filed Jul 10 and Aug 17, 2026 in N.D. Cal., no lead plaintiff yet; two derivative suits Sep 18 and 28, 2026. Intuit says it cannot estimate loss. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000045/intu-20261031.htm (2026-11-19).
- Fact: a Bloomberg Law report describes the securities suit as alleging overstated TurboTax competitive strength before the weak 2026 season. Source: https://news.bloomberglaw.com/securities-law/intuit-investor-sues-over-underwhelming-2026-tax-season-growth (2026-07). Allegation only.
- Means: government risk shrank; private-litigation risk grew. The securities suits echo the thesis's own weak point (TurboTax share). Dollar exposure is not estimable from filings. Confidence: high on the note, low on outcomes.
