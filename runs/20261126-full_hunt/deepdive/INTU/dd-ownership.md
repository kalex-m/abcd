## Findings

**F1. Only open-market buy since the -60% fall: one director, small.**
Fact: Vasant Prabhu (director) bought 1,250 shares at $309.45 (2026-05-22) and 500 at $309.72 (2026-05-26); Form 4 flag "10b5-1 plan" = 0; his proxy holding is 2,495 incl. 745 vested RSUs, so the 1,750 are the purchased shares. Zero other code "P" trades in any Form 4 from 2025-06-01 to 2026-11-25 (451 non-derivative lines parsed: 227 M exercises, 154 S sales, 67 F tax withholdings, 2 P, 1 G).
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000162828026038218/wk-form4_1779830291.xml (filed 2026-05-26); proxy https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000053/intu-20261125.htm (as of 2026-10-31).
Means: a modest vote of confidence, but about 0.002% of the company. No buying at $270 (today's price); the CEO and CFO have not bought at all. Not the cluster the thesis would want. Confidence: high.

**F2. Selling was plan-driven and concentrated at far higher prices.**
Fact: Cook (founder, director) 110 plan sales, 557,710 shares, ~$376M, 2025-06-09 to 2025-12-31 filing dates (2026-01-02 last), prices $632-$772. CEO Goodarzi sold 41,000 shares at ~$650 on 2026-01-07 (~$26.7M, plan). All Cook, Goodarzi and Dalzell sales carry the Form 4 10b5-1 box = 1. Cook still holds 5,637,432 shares (2.13%).
Source: Form 4 e.g. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000162828025059105/wk-form4_1767128994.xml (2025-12-30); CEO sale accession 0001628280-26-001365 (filed 2026-01-08); proxy table above.
Means: insiders sold most of what they sold before the collapse, on pre-set schedules. This is neither a signal that insiders saw the fall coming (plans were adopted earlier, e.g. Cook's on 2025-09-03) nor evidence they are dumping now. Confidence: high on facts, medium on interpretation.

**F3. Cook terminated his selling plan after the fall.**
Fact: 10-Q for the quarter to 2026-04-30 Item 5: on 2026-03-14 Cook terminated a plan (adopted 2025-09-03) for up to 1,843,868 shares (Dec 3, 2025 to Oct 9, 2026). About 1.29M shares were left unsold. In the same filing the CFO, strategy chief and general counsel also terminated plans on 2026-03-12 (12,940, 15,000 and 25,077 shares).
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000025/intu-20260430.htm (filed 2026-05-20).
Means: the largest seller stopped when the price fell. Mildly supportive, but it is a stop in selling, not buying. Confidence: high.

**F4. New executive sale plans start in December at about 55% below the old prices.**
Fact: Q1 FY27 10-Q Item 5: 2026-09-11 Goodarzi (Chairman/CEO) adopted a plan for up to 94,477 shares, selling 2026-12-11 to 2027-10-08; 2026-09-16 CFO Aujla up to 18,600 shares (2026-12-16 to 2027-07-09); 2026-09-16 Hanebrink up to 70,394 shares (2026-12-16 to 2027-12-16). Goodarzi's holdings: 262,440 options/vested RSUs plus 32,306 trust shares (proxy), so his plan is about a third of his total. No plan adoption or termination was disclosed in the 10-K for the quarter to 2026-07-31.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000045/intu-20261031.htm (filed 2026-11-19); 10-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm (filed 2026-09-09).
Means: the CEO is scheduling sales at a depressed price while a catalyst window (Feb and May 2027 results, Mailchimp close) is ahead. Plans are usually for tax or diversification, but a CEO adopting a sale plan a few months after a 60% drop is a negative for the "management sees value" angle. Not a break. Confidence: medium.
