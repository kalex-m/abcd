# INTU (Intuit) deep-dive focus

**Status note:** promoted to Survived by the main thread (see `runs/20261126-full_hunt/STATUS_REVIEW.md`); the triage itself said Watch. Test hardest where the triage hesitated.

**Thesis under test:** the market prices all of Intuit as an AI seat-compression victim (stock about -60% from its 52-week high), but ~56% of segment operating income is Global Business Solutions (QuickBooks Online, payments and money movement, Mailchimp being sold), growing ~15%, sold per customer rather than per enterprise seat. At $271.72 (EV ~$73.3B) the price needs only ~2% a year owner FCF growth for 10 years vs ~22% over 5 years. Catalysts: Q2 FY27 results (late Feb 2027), Mailchimp sale closing (early 2027) with a $3.3B buyback, Q3 FY27 tax-season results (May 2027).

**Known risks (the Zoom test on Consumer):** Credit Karma FY27 growth guide cut from +11-13% to +3-7% with a faster consumer shift to AI discovery; TurboTax cutting price to regain filer share; fiscal Q2 Consumer revenue guided down mid-single digits; unit and customer counts not disclosed; IRS Direct File; AI tax assistants.
