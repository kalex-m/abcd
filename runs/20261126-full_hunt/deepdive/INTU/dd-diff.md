# INTU dd-diff (what changed between versions), run 20261126-full_hunt, as of 2026-11-28

## Summary for main thread
1. **Long-term GBS target cut is verified.** "15-20% annual growth" (set and reiterated 2024-25) became "10-15% CAGR over three years" on the Aug 25, 2026 call and Sep 17, 2026 Investor Day. Consumer fell to 4-8%.
2. **Recasts matter.** The Mailchimp carve-out cuts GBS's share of segment profit from 61% to 56%; non-GAAP EPS now includes stock pay (FY26 $24.27 became $18.63). Like for like, GBS growth slowed from 20% to 15%, and its Q1 profit grew 12%.
3. **Guidance:** 9 of 9 quarters beat the EPS top end, but the revenue cushion shrank to 1 point. Credit Karma FY27 was cut within three months.

Thesis impact: weakens

## Findings
Source keys (S#) are listed under Sources.

**F1. Long-term GBS target.** Fact: "15 to 20 percent" annual GBS revenue growth (online paying customers +5-10%, ARPC +10-20%) was reiterated on the Q4 FY24 call [S22], Sep 2024 Investor Day [S17], the Q4 FY25 call [S21] and Sep 2025 Investor Day [S16]. (The 15-20% cited on the May 2026 call was TurboTax Live, not GBS.) On Aug 25, 2026 the CFO said GBS would grow "at a CAGR of 10 to 15 percent over the next three years" and Consumer 4-8%, "prudent" on each lever [S18]. The Sep 17, 2026 deck repeats this and adds "at least high-teens" non-GAAP EPS growth [S15]. Prior decks [S16, S17] show no quantified EPS growth target (nothing found). Meaning: midpoint falls from 17.5% to 12.5%. The new range sits on the ex-Mailchimp base and is a 3-year average, so it is not a like-for-like comparison, but the direction is clear, and FY27 guidance (13-14%) already sits in the top half. Confidence: high.

**F2. Mailchimp is not "held for sale" yet.** The 10-Q says assets were not classed as held for sale at Oct 31, 2026 because the board approved the deal after quarter end; sale price $3.68B ($3.31B cash, $368M Bending Spoons stock) [S14]. All guidance still includes Mailchimp (FY27 $1,256-1,266M) and will be re-issued at close [S10]. Expect another recast (likely discontinued operations) in early 2027. Confidence: high.

**F3. Recast log.** See the restatement table. The Aug 2025 recast moved $606M (FY25) of Consumer costs into unallocated corporate expense, raising Consumer segment profit; the Nov 2026 recast moved $355M (FY26) of corporate cost into Mailchimp. Segment profit is a moving measure. Confidence: high.

**F4. GBS share of segment profit (the thesis "56%").** FY26 GBS segment OI $9,887M of $16,195M = 61.0% in the 10-K [S12]; $8,928M of $15,840M = 56.4% after the carve-out [S11]. FY25: 62.2% (FY25 10-K [S13]) → 59.5% (Aug 2025 recast) → 54.5% (Nov 2026 recast). Computed by me from the cited tables. The 56% figure is on the post-recast basis and holds only while Mailchimp ($604M) stays in the denominator; ex-Mailchimp, GBS is 58.6% of GBS+Consumer. Confidence: high.

**F5. GBS growth is slowing like for like.** Recast GBS growth by quarter: Q1 FY26 20%, Q2 21%, Q3 17%, Q4 15%, Q1 FY27 15% [S11]. Q1 FY27 GBS segment OI $2,353M vs $2,105M (+11.8%) on revenue +15.3%; margin 76.3% vs 78.7% (my calculation) [S11]. Q2 FY27 guide: GBS "low to mid-teens" [S19]. On the old definition (with Mailchimp), FY27 guidance implies about +11.4% to +12.1% versus +16% in FY26 (my calculation from S9, S11). Meaning: the recast does not flatter the continuing business, but headline "15%" is already a lower number than the 18-20% of last year. Confidence: high.
