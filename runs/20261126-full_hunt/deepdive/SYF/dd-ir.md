# SYF dd-ir: company IR layer (run 20261126-full_hunt)

## Summary for main thread
1. Public targets (Feb 2023 framework, repeated in the Q4'25 deck) are only half met. ROA beat (2.9% vs ~2.5%+) and CET1 is above target (12.9% vs ~11%), but ROTCE (~28%+ target) was 25.8% in 2025 and 24.3% for 9M26. Efficiency (35.9% vs ~32-33%), NIM, loan growth and double-digit EPS growth all missed.
2. RSA (retailer share) protection is real but partial. Partners took roughly 32-49% of the swing in net interest income minus charge-offs over 2024-26. The 10-K says expense increases "may not necessarily" cut RSA payments, and co-brand loans (36%, +22%) sometimes use royalties instead.
3. Q3 net earnings fell 17%, not just 6% EPS. Half the drop is a +$123M (+10%) expense rise, not only the reserve swing. Credit guidance has been conservative and raised; revenue and efficiency guidance missed in 2025.

Thesis impact: neutral

## Findings

**F1. Targets vs delivery.** The Feb 2023 investor deck sets long-term targets: loan growth 7-10%, NIM ~16%, NCO ~5.5-6.0%, RSA ~4.0-4.5% of loans, efficiency ~32-33%, CET1 ~11.0%, ROA ~2.5%+, ROTCE ~28%+, double-digit EPS growth. The Q4'25 deck still shows ROA ~2.5%+ and ROTCE ~28%+. Delivery: ROA 3.0% (2025), 2.9% (Q3'26); ROTCE 27.5% (2024), 25.8% (2025), 24.3% (9M26); efficiency 34.3% (2025), 35.8% (9M26); NIM 15.24% (2025), 15.36% (9M26); loans $103.3B (+3% y/y); EPS $8.55 (2024), $9.28 (2025, +8.5%), ~$9.50 guided 2026 (+2%). NCO 5.65% (2025) landed in target; 9M26 5.21% is better than target.
Sources: https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_19037aad8568638f284b4dd8c495fb2f/synchrony/db/3583/33295/pdf/synchrony-investor-slides-final.pdf (2023-02-01); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000003/a4q25earningspresentatio.htm (2026-01-27); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000040/financialtables3q26.htm (2026-10-20).
Meaning: the "25% ROTCE earned" in the thesis is below management's own 28% goal and falling, so the price-implied ~14% is still far below earnings, but the earnings level is lower than the company's public ambition. Confidence: high.

**F2. ROTCE history was recast, and CET1 was lifted.** In Q2'26 the company changed how internal-use capitalized software appears on the balance sheet and recast prior periods. Tangible common equity for Q3'25 went from $13,660M to $14,505M; tangible book per share from $37.93 to $40.28; Q3'25 ROTCE from 30.6% to 28.9%; Q4'25 ROTCE from 21.8% to 20.3%; Q3'25 CET1 from 13.7% to 14.3%. The 2025 full-year 25.8% and 2024 27.5% ROTCE in decks are on the old basis. Recast 2025 full-year ROTCE is not published in what I read; my arithmetic from 9M25 (25.7%) and Q4 (20.3%) gives about 24%.
Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171225000256/financialtables3q25.htm (2025-10-15); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000040/financialtables3q26.htm and /earningsrelease3q26.htm (2026-10-20).
Meaning: ROTCE gap to the 28% target is wider than old-basis figures suggest; the 12.9% vs 14.3% CET1 fall is like for like. Confidence: high (restated figures), medium (my 2025 estimate).

**F3. RSA mechanics, quantified.** The 10-K says most large-partner programs pay partners a share of program profit above a contractual threshold. Program profit is revenue less "agreed" expenses (including provision), and the agreed measures may differ from actual expenses, so higher actual expenses may not cut payments. Co-brand cards sometimes carry royalties instead of RSA. Co-brand loans are $37.5B (36% of $103.3B, +22% y/y). My arithmetic from the supplements: RSA/average loans was 3.86% (2023), 3.35% (2024), 3.99% (2025), 4.21% (9M26, annualised); Q3'26 4.3%. Change in RSA divided by change in (net interest income minus NCOs): 2024 vs 2023 about 32%; 2025 vs 2024 about 49%; 9M26 vs 9M25 about 40%. In 2024, NCOs rose about $1.8B while RSA fell only $254M.
Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000006/syf-20251231.htm (10-K, 2026-02-06); financial tables 4Q24 .../000160171225000041/financialtables4q24.htm, 3Q25, 3Q26 (links as above); deck https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000040/a3q26earningspresentatio.htm (2026-10-20).
Meaning: in a downturn, partners absorb perhaps a third to a half of the loss swing, not most of it. The reserve build (CECL) hits SYF in full up front; RSA follows charge-offs (Q3 RSA rose $72M while provision rose $120M). The company does not disclose the share of loans under RSA. The cushion is smaller than "partners share losses" implies. Confidence: medium (derived, not disclosed).

**F4. Q3 profit drop is half expenses.** Pre-tax earnings fell $248M: net interest income +$89M, RSA -$72M, provision -$120M (reserve build $30M vs $152M release), other income -$22M, other expense -$123M (+10%, "higher operational losses, technology investments and marketing"). Net earnings -17%; EPS -6% only because shares fell 12.8%. Efficiency 35.9% vs 32.6%. Guidance: Q4'26 other expense about equal to Q3.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000040/earningsrelease3q26.htm and deck (2026-10-20).
Meaning: the triage line "reserve build, not worse credit" is half right; cost growth is the other half and is guided to persist. Confidence: high.

**F5. Guidance history.** 2025 initial to final (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct 2025 decks): NCO 5.8-6.1% to 5.6-5.7%, actual 5.65% (hit, conservative). RSA/loans 3.60-3.85% to 3.95-4.05%, actual 3.99%. Net revenue $15.2-15.7B to $15.0-15.1B, actual $14.98B (below the final range). Efficiency 31.5-32.5% to 33.0-33.5%, actual 34.3% (about 33.8% excluding $70M Q4 notable items; still above range). 2026 (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct 2026): EPS $9.10-9.50, then $9.10-9.50, $9.25-9.50, ~$9.50; NCO ~5.5-6.0% then <5.5%. Implied Q4'26 EPS about $1.98 (9M26 $7.52) vs $2.04 a year ago.
Sources: decks .../000160171225000041/a4q24earningspresentatio.htm, .../000160171225000138/a1q25..., .../000160171225000228/a2q25..., .../000160171225000256/a3q25..., .../000160171226000003/a4q25..., .../000160171226000013/a1q26..., .../000160171226000030/a2q26..., .../000160171226000040/a3q26earningspresentatio.htm (filing dates 2025-01-28 to 2026-10-20).
Meaning: management is conservative on credit and has raised EPS guidance twice; it missed on revenue and expense in 2025. The implied Q4 EPS is below Q4'25. Confidence: high.

**F6. Monthly credit supplement needs adjusting.** The 8-K of 2026-11-10 shows Oct 2026 NCO 4.4% vs 5.0%, but Oct 2026 had 25 charge-off cycle dates vs 28 a year earlier. The company's adjusted NCO rate is 4.6% vs 5.3%, still better. 30+ delinquency 4.4% (Oct 2025 4.5%), up from 4.2% in Jun-Jul 2026.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000045/creditstatsfinancialtables.htm (2026-10-31).
Meaning: the triage's "4.4% vs 5.0%" overstates the gain by about 0.1pt; direction holds. Confidence: high.

**F7. Partner renewals.** The 2025 annual report letter says the top five partners are renewed through 2030 and beyond, and 22 of the 25 largest programs (97% of their interest and fees) through 2028 or beyond. Lowe's commercial co-brand card was added (press release 2026-04-30); Walmart/OnePay launched Sep 2025 as the fastest-growing new program.
Sources: https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_19037aad8568638f284b4dd8c495fb2f/synchrony/db/3619/33984/annual_report/Synchrony+2025+AR+WRAP+FINAL+WEB+POST+04292026+%28Annual+Report%29.pdf (2026-04-29); https://investors.synchrony.com/news-events/financial-news/detail/571/synchrony-expands-partnership-with-lowes-as-new-issuer-of-co-brand-credit-card-for-home-improvement-professionals.
Meaning: reduces partner-loss risk through 2028. Confidence: medium (company statement, no contract text).

**F8. Capital.** CET1 12.9% vs ~11% target = about $2.0B excess on $105.7B risk-weighted assets (my arithmetic). Q3 buybacks $1.1B (about 130% of net earnings); shares 360.1M to 311.4M (-13.5%) y/y; implied Q3 repurchase price about $79 (my arithmetic), above the ~$69 implied by 1.61x P/TBV.
Source: earnings release and financial tables 3Q26 (2026-10-20).
Meaning: buybacks are shrinking CET1 about 0.3-0.4pt a quarter; at that pace the target is reached within about 5 quarters. Confidence: medium.
