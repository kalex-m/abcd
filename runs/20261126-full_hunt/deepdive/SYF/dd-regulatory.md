# SYF dd-regulatory (run 20261126-full_hunt, as of 2026-11-27)

## Summary for main thread
1. **The card-rate-cap risk got materially worse on 2026-11-21.** Trump said he would work with the incoming Democratic Congress on a 10% cap. Democrats take both chambers, and Sen. Warren is expected to chair Senate Banking. The two cap bills (S. 381 / H.R. 1944) are still only in committee, but the political block that stopped them is gone. The triage did not cover this.
2. **No pending deal, consent order or named litigation.** The legal note is generic and unchanged between the FY2025 10-K and the Q2 2026 10-Q. The old 2014 CFPB order was terminated in 2025. The $8 late-fee rule is vacated, so it is a closed risk.
3. **Management says a cap would hit partners, not just Synchrony.** The retailer share arrangement (RSA) cushions credit losses, not a cut to the yield on loans.

Thesis impact: weakens (the "mistake" holds on credit, but the rate-cap catalyst now cuts the wrong way and may explain part of the discount).

## Findings

**1. Rate-cap politics escalated on 2026-11-21.**
- Fact: Trump said he would work with the incoming Democratic Congress to cap card rates at 10%. Warren (expected Banking chair) and Waters back caps. Hawley (R) urged passing his bill. The report states that Democrats will control both chambers.
- Source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trump-vows-to-work-with-democrats-to-cap-credit-card-interest-rates-at-10 (2026-11-21)
- Meaning: a cap becomes more plausible in 2027. Synchrony is among the lenders most exposed, because store cards carry high APRs.
- Confidence: high on the statement; medium on odds of passage.

**2. The bills have not advanced.**
- Fact: S. 381 and H.R. 1944 would cap APRs at 10% through Jan 2031. Both were referred to committee and had no hearing or vote in the cited coverage. Trump's own version is a one-year cap. A separate Roger Marshall bill was reported to cover only banks over $100B in assets (which includes Synchrony); I could not confirm its text or status.
- Sources: https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/381/text (page found by search; the history page returned 403); https://www.icba.org/documents/d/asset-library-45247/legislative-update-credit-card-rate-caps-july-2026- (2026-07); https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-03-10_IF12861_5df7ae29ff4e4691d85bfb03e856420de68e254d.html (2026-03-10, CRS: no general national cap exists)
- Meaning: there is no law today. The risk is a 2027 event, not a Dec 2026 one. Congress.gov status was not directly readable, so the "still in committee" claim rests on secondary coverage dated 2026-11-23.
- Confidence: medium.

**3. Synchrony's own position and exposure.**
- Fact: CEO Brian Doubles said on 2026-01-27 that a 10% cap would force issuers to cut credit, hit lower-income users, and be "very bad" for retail partners. Synchrony supports about 400,000 small and mid-size businesses. S&P Global (2026-02) found card loans at 92.4% of Synchrony's loan book, the second-highest share among lenders it analysed. Shares fell from $88.21 (Jan 9 open) to $72.63 (Jan 30 close).
- Sources: https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/synchrony-says-retailers-would-also-be-hurt-by-10-rate-cap (2026-02-02); S&P article found by search (https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2026/2/credit-card-lenders-interest-rates-not-at-risk-for-now-97935383; 403 on fetch, so the figures come from the search summary and are unverified).
- Meaning: the "late-cycle casualty" discount in the triage may partly be rate-cap pricing, not a credit-cycle misreading. The thesis should say so. A Risk.net headline (NII down over 40% at top lenders under a cap) could not be opened (page error), so no quantified impact is used here.
- Confidence: medium.

**4. The CFPB $8 late-fee rule is dead.**
- Fact: N.D. Texas vacated it on 2025-04-15 by consent judgment, after the CFPB conceded it exceeded CARD Act authority. The CARD Act safe harbour (about $41) applies again. Synchrony's 10-K and 10-Q still list the vacated rule in forward-looking statements. Late fees were $558M in Q3 2026 vs $592M a year earlier (from triage), a drop that is behaviour, not a rule.
- Sources: https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/04/cfpb-credit-card-late-fees-rule-vacated-by-texas-district-court (2025-04); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000043/syf-20260930.htm (2026-09-30)
- Meaning: no live federal fee ceiling. A new rulemaking is possible in a future CFPB but nothing is found. Confidence: high. (I did not check for any post-2025 CFPB rulemaking at the primary source.)

**5. Consent orders: none live; the 2014 order is terminated.**
- Fact: the CFPB terminated the 2014 Synchrony Bank (ex-GE Capital Retail Bank) consent order in 2025, citing Executive Order 14281 (disparate-impact theories). The termination order records at least $259M redress and a $3.5M penalty were paid. I found no 2026 CFPB, OCC or state attorney general action against Synchrony.
- Source: https://files-prod.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_synchrony_order-terminating-consent-order_2025-05.pdf (2025-05); https://www.consumerfinance.gov/enforcement/actions/synchrony-bank-fka-ge-capital-retail-bank
- Meaning: clean enforcement slate. Searches of OCC and state AG sites were not run directly. Confidence: medium-high.

**6. Legal-proceedings note: unchanged boilerplate.**
- Fact: the FY2025 10-K Note 18 (p.150) and the Q2 2026 10-Q Note 15 (p.61) use the same wording. Neither names a case, regulator or accrual. Both say management does not expect a material adverse effect on financial condition or liquidity, while warning that one matter could be material to a given period's earnings. The Q2 10-Q Item 1A says no material changes to the 2025 10-K risk factors. The Q3 10-Q note could not be read (HTML fetch truncated before the notes).
- Sources: https://investors.synchrony.com/filings-regulatory/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001601712-26-000006/0001601712-26-000006.pdf (2025-12-31); https://investors.synchrony.com/filings-regulatory/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001601712-26-000033/0001601712-26-000033.pdf (2026-06-30)
- Meaning: no litigation overhang. Year-over-year change: none found. FY2024 note was not read, so the comparison is FY2025 vs mid-2026 only. Confidence: high for these two documents.

**7. Credit Card Competition Act (swipe-fee routing) is a separate, smaller risk.**
- Fact: S. 3623 was reintroduced 2026-01-13 (Welch, Durbin, Marshall). It was referred to Banking with no markup. A March 2026 attempt to attach it to the housing bill failed. A NACS item (2026-08-11) reports new cosponsors. Trump has voiced support.
- Sources: https://www.durbin.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/durbin-marshall-reintroduce-the-credit-card-competition-act (2026-01-13); https://www.convenience.org/stay-current/news/2026/august/11/cash-or-credit-two-nacs-gr-priorities-make-progress (2026-08-11)
- Meaning: it mainly hits Visa/Mastercard and large rewards issuers. Synchrony's private-label cards run on its own rails, so the direct effect is likely small, but co-brand cards ($37.5B per triage) use networks. Not quantified. Confidence: low-medium.

**8. State actions: nothing enacted.**
- Fact: Arizona SB 1623 (15% cap, $10 late fee) was introduced; passage not confirmed. A New York opt-out bill is not expected to move before 2027. A bill to block states capping out-of-state bank rates was introduced (ABA Banking Journal, 2026-02).
- Sources: https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/57leg/2r/bills/sb1623p.htm ; https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/08/31/new-york-legislator-introduces-didmca-opt-out-bill-but-action-will-have-to-wait-until-2027/ (2026-08-31)
- Meaning: state usury is a minor risk now. Synchrony Bank is a federally chartered savings association (OCC-regulated, per the 10-K); how its rate authority interacts with state caps was not checked. Confidence: low.

**9. Capital-regulation context (not a docket, but a live review).**
- Fact: the Fed held stress capital buffers until 2027 at the earliest. Synchrony stays at 2.5% and gets its next buffer in 2028. At 2026-06-30, CET1 was 13.2% (Bank 14.4%). The Series C preferred ($500M) was issued in June 2026. A Fed comment-letter page (FR-2026-0008) and a Synchrony meeting record (2026-02-24) exist on federalreserve.gov but were not read.
- Source: https://investors.synchrony.com/filings-regulatory/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001601712-26-000033/0001601712-26-000033.pdf (2026-06-30)
- Meaning: no capital docket threatens buybacks now. Confidence: medium.

**10. Side note on fraud losses.** Fraud-related operational losses were $166M in H1 2026 vs $75M in H1 2025 (FY2025: $189M; FY2023: $288M). This helps explain the efficiency-ratio slip. It is not a regulatory finding. Source: the Q2 2026 10-Q p.60 (link above). Confidence: high.
