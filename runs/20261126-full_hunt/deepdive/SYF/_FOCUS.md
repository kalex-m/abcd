# SYF (Synchrony Financial) deep-dive focus

**Thesis under test (from triage):** the market prices Synchrony as a late-cycle card casualty (shares -22% from the high; P/TBV 1.61x implies ~14% ROTCE) while credit is better than a year ago (Oct 30+ delinquency 4.4% vs 4.5%; charge-offs 4.4% vs 5.0%), reserves are ~10% of loans, and retailer share arrangements (RSAs) let partners absorb part of any loss increase. Q3 EPS -6% came from a reserve build vs a prior-year release, not worse credit. ROTCE 25% in Q3. Catalysts: monthly credit 8-K (~Dec 10), Q4 results and 2027 outlook (late Jan 2027), continued buybacks.

**Known risks:** 3 partners each >10% of interest and fees, top 5 = 54%; CET1 fell 14.3% to 12.9%; Q3 efficiency worsened; credit-card interest-rate cap proposals and late-fee rules; BNPL/agentic checkout shifting where credit is offered; the bad-year ROTCE (~18% in 2023) was a rough triage estimate.

**Layer tests**
- dd-edgar: monthly credit 8-Ks (trend of delinquency and NCO vs seasonal norms over 24 months), risk-factor diff 10-K 2025 vs 2024, CAMs (allowance for credit losses), partner-concentration disclosures, any 8-K Items 1.01/2.05/5.02.
- dd-ir: investor-day targets (ROTCE, NCO range, efficiency) vs delivery; RSA mechanics in supplements; guidance history.
- dd-transcripts: management wording on credit, partner renewals (and any lost partner), Walmart/OnePay ramp, buyback pace; promises vs delivery.
- dd-regulatory: CFPB late-fee rule status, federal credit-card rate-cap bills, state actions, consent orders, legal-proceedings note year over year.
- dd-credit: funding stack (deposits, securitisations, unsecured debt) and maturity ladder; CET1 vs stress capital buffer and target; buybacks and dividends vs earnings; share count.
- dd-ownership: open-market Form 4 trades vs 10b5-1; 13F changes by large holders; short interest.
- dd-peers: COF, AXP, Bread Financial (BFH): same credit trends? Partners' filings (Lowe's, Amazon, PayPal, Walmart, Verizon etc.) naming Synchrony and any program changes.
- dd-diff: guidance given vs actual (NCO, NIM, efficiency) for the last 8 quarters; any restated numbers; wording diff across calls.
