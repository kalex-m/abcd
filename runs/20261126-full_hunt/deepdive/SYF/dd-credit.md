# SYF dd-credit (run 20261126-full_hunt, as of 2026-11-27; latest balance sheet 2026-09-30)

## Summary for main thread
1. **Buybacks are running ahead of capital generation.** In the year to Q3 2026, earnings added 3.3 points of CET1 (the core capital ratio) while buybacks took 3.8 and dividends 0.5. Capital returned in 9M 2026 was about 125% of net earnings. CET1 is 12.9% against a 7.0% floor, so there is about $6.2B of room, more than the $4.6B left on the buyback plan.
2. **The 14.3% in the thesis is the Q3 2025 figure on a recast basis.** The like-for-like Dec 2025 figure is 13.5%, so the drop since year-end is 0.6 points.
3. **Funding is 83% deposits, with a 2027 repricing wall.** $31.5B of certificates of deposit (CDs), about two-thirds of all CDs, mature in 2027. Securitisation triggers are far away (excess spread 13-18% against a 0% trigger). S&P outlook turned Positive.

Thesis impact: supports

## Findings
1. **Funding stack.** Fact: deposits $82.95B, securitised borrowings $8.92B, senior and subordinated unsecured notes $8.11B, total about $100.0B. Deposits are 83% of funding. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000043/syf-20260930.htm (2026-09-30). Meaning: a bank-style, mostly insured deposit base, not a wholesale-funded lender. Confidence: high.
2. **Deposit mix and cost.** Fact: Q3 average CDs $43.3B (52.5% of interest-bearing deposits, 4.0%), savings/money market $32.6B (39.5%, 3.3%), brokered $6.6B (8.0%, 4.1%). Total deposit cost 3.7% vs 4.0% a year ago. CDs rose from 49.3% to 52.5% of the mix. Uninsured deposits $7.1B (about 8.6%). Source: same 10-Q (2026-09-30). Meaning: cost is falling, but the mix is shifting to the costlier, term-locked CDs. Rate-sensitive money is less flighty, though it reprices at each maturity. Confidence: high.
3. **CD maturity wall in 2027.** Fact: CDs mature $8.4B (rest of 2026), $31.5B (2027), $3.8B (2028), $1.5B (2029), $2.1B (2030), $0.55B (after). Weighted average maturity is about one year. Source: same 10-Q, Note 7 (2026-09-30). Meaning: this is the biggest single ladder item. With the 10-year at 4.85% and the Fed leaning to hike (regime brief), rollovers may not reprice lower. A funding-cost tailwind should not be assumed. I found no CD retention data in the filings read. Confidence: high on the ladder, low on the repricing effect.
4. **Securitisation and unsecured ladder.** Fact: securitised $8.925B (under one year $3.9B, which includes $750M repaid in Oct 2026; one to three years $5.0B; nothing after). Unsecured notes $8.15B by final maturity: 2027 $1.6B (Aug $600M bank notes, Dec $1.0B 3.95%), 2029 $1.15B, 2030 $1.35B, 2031 $1.55B, 2032 $0.75B, 2033 $0.75B (7.25% subordinated), 2036 $0.5B, 2037 $0.5B. Nothing matures in 2028. Weighted rate 5.24%. Issued in 2026: $750M (Feb) and $1.1B (Jul). Source: same 10-Q, Notes 5 and 8 (2026-09-30). Meaning: wholesale maturities are small and spread out. 2027 needs about $1.6B unsecured plus about $5B securitised against $19.5B of liquid assets. Confidence: high.
5. **Liquidity.** Fact: liquid assets $19.5B (15.9% of assets, up from $16.6B at Dec 2025); Fed discount window capacity $13.0B; undrawn securitisation $2.6B ($2.1B committed). Sources: same 10-Q; release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000040/earningsrelease3q26.htm (2026-09-30). Meaning: more than the 2026-27 non-CD wholesale maturities. It would cover all the CDs only if most of them roll. Confidence: high.
6. **Covenants and triggers.** Fact: the indentures (EX-4) carry only structural covenants. They restrict liens on the Bank's voting stock and any merger or sale that leaves the Bank under 80% controlled. Compliance confirmed, with no financial-ratio covenants described. For securitisations, an early amortisation event follows if excess spread falls below zero. Dec 2025 three-month excess spread was SYNCT about 16.4-17.0%, SFT 13.0%, SYNIT 17.9%. No early amortisation has occurred. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000006/syf-20251231.htm (2025-12-31), and the 10-Q. Meaning: the real "covenants" are regulatory capital and these triggers. Headroom is wide, because charge-offs of 4-5% are far from consuming a 13%+ spread. Confidence: high (10-K) and medium (Sep 2026 spread not read).
7. **CET1 headroom vs stress capital buffer (SCB).** Fact: CET1 $13.58B, 12.9% of $105.7B risk-weighted assets. Requirement 4.5% + SCB 2.5% = 7.0%. Excess is about 5.9 points, or about $6.2B. SYF took its first Fed stress test in 2026 and stays at the 2.5% SCB (the floor). The next test is 2028 and the next change would take effect 1 Jan 2029. Tier 1 14.5%, leverage 12.7%, total 16.5%. Bank-level CET1 14.3%. Source: the 10-Q capital section (2026-09-30); final Fed rule of 2026-09-30 moves SCB effective dates (same filing). Meaning: even a severe loss year would not threaten the 7.0% floor. The real constraint is management's own comfort level. A stated CET1 target: nothing_found in the 10-Q, release or presentation (only an RSA 4.0-4.5% range appears). Confidence: high.
8. **The 14.3% to 12.9% fall is partly a recast.** Fact: 14.3% is Q3 2025 (recast). Dec 2025 CET1 was 12.6% as originally filed and 13.5% recast (capitalised software presentation change); Q2 2026 was 13.2%. The Q3-2025-to-Q3-2026 bridge: +3.3 (earnings), -3.8 (buybacks), -0.5 (dividends), -0.4 (RWA growth) = -1.4. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000040/a3q26earningspresentatio.htm (2026-09-30); Q2 10-Q https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601712/000160171226000033/syf-20260630.htm; 10-K (2025-12-31). Meaning: the triage's "14.3% to 12.9%" compares different bases. The year-to-date fall is 0.6 points, and a fall is a choice, not a shock. Confidence: high.
9. **Buybacks and dividends vs earnings.** Fact: 9M 2026 buybacks $2.85B (38.3M shares), common dividends $314M, preferred $70M, against net earnings $2.58B, so total returned is about 125% of earnings. Q3 alone is about 139% ($1.1B + $110M + $28M against $892M). Quarterly average buyback price was about $72.0 (Q1), $72.6 (Q2), $78.0 (Q3); 2025 averaged about $66.7 ($2.913B for 43.7M shares). The price today is $68.73 (triage), below the Q3 average paid. $4.6B remains on the $6.5B no-expiry programme (4 quarters at Q3 pace). Dividend raised to $0.34. Series C preferred ($500M, 7.25%) was issued in June 2026 and adds to Tier 1 but not CET1. Source: 10-Q (2026-09-30) and 10-K buyback table (2025-12-31). Meaning: at the Q3 pace CET1 trends toward about 12% in a year. That is still far above 7.0%, but the cushion that buys shares in a slowdown is being spent now. Buying at about $78 in Q3 was the highest price of the three quarters. Confidence: high.
10. **Share count.** Fact: 311.4M shares at 2026-10-16 (cover page), against 347M (2025) and 388M (2024) per triage. Q3 EPS is $2.71 basic (net to common about $864M, so about 319M average shares). Source: 10-Q cover (2026-10-16). Meaning: about 10% fewer shares in 2026 and about 20% in two years. EPS is supported by buybacks when net earnings fell 17% in Q3 (892 vs 1,077). Dilution from stock comp was not quantified. Confidence: high on count, low on dilution.
11. **Ratings.** Fact: S&P senior unsecured BBB- (Positive outlook, changed from Stable in the 10-K); Bank BBB (Positive). Fitch BBB / BBB (Stable). Preferred BB- at both. Moody's is not shown in the table. Source: 10-Q (2026-09-30) vs 10-K (2025-12-31). Meaning: no downgrade; S&P is leaning up. The holding-company debt is the weakest link, at the bottom of investment grade at S&P. Confidence: high.
12. **Off-balance-sheet.** Fact: no material off-balance-sheet arrangements or guarantees. Unused customer card lines are about $455B (Dec 2025: $440B), which the company says will not be drawn all at once. Source: 10-Q (2026-09-30). Meaning: the only large contingent item is unused lines, normal for a card lender. Confidence: high.
13. **Pensions.** nothing_found: no defined-benefit pension liability appears in the 10-K or 10-Q text searched. Confidence: medium (keyword search only).
