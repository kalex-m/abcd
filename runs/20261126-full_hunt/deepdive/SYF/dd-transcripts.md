# SYF dd-transcripts (run 20261126-full_hunt, written 2026-11-27)

## Summary for main thread
1. Management itself says the "credit is better than a year ago" read is a lagging view. Since Jan 2026 it has warned that Walmart/OnePay losses build as accounts age (about three vintages), and on the Q3 2026 call it said charge-offs may be higher in 2027 and the usual Q4 reserve decline will be smaller.
2. Offsetting that, management says "double digit" EPS growth is still the 2027 framework, backed by buybacks. Guidance delivery on charge-offs and EPS has been good; delivery on efficiency and operating leverage has been poor.
3. Analysts have repeatedly asked about low-income borrowers and the CET1 path. Answers were by credit-score band, or none. The 11% CET1 target has been repeated since Apr 2025 with no timeline.

Thesis impact: weakens (mildly; the mistake still stands on price, but "credit better, not worse" is incomplete)

## Findings

**1. Walmart loss build is now explicit (wording shift: from upside to cost).**
- Jul 2025: management said Walmart/OnePay would not matter much in 2025 and its loss content should be well below the old program's (transcript garbled; low confidence on exact figure). Source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/07/22/synchrony-syf-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript/ (2025-07-22).
- Jan 2026: fastest-growing new program, but also a named source of early loss pressure in 2026. Source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/01/27/synchrony-syf-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/ (2026-01-27).
- Oct 2026: losses "begin to come online" as credit actions season; loss rate builds from 2026 into 2027; Q4 2026 ends the first vintage. Source: https://www.webull.com/news/15781703289185280 (2026-10-20; third-party transcript with name and figure errors).
- Means: the y/y charge-off improvement (4.79% vs 5.16% in Q3) flatters the forward path. The thesis leans on "credit is better"; management says the direction of losses turns up in 2027. Confidence: medium-high (consistent across three calls).

**2. Q3 reserve build was partly seasoning, not only a swing from last year's release.**
- Q3 2026 build of $30M vs a $152M release a year earlier; management said not to expect the usual Q4 coverage decline, citing seasoning and credit actions. Source: same Webull transcript (2026-10-20).
- Means: supports the triage claim that the EPS dip came from the reserve swing, but weakens the idea that reserves are a cushion that will release. Confidence: medium.

**3. 2027 promise to track: double-digit EPS growth despite higher losses.**
- Management declined 2027 guidance but described a framework (NII growing with loans, seasoning benefit, lapping operational losses, buybacks) that supports "double digit EPS growth." It also said deposit betas will be higher than in the last hiking cycle and that one more rate hike is expected in December; it will find offsets. Source: Webull Q3 2026 (2026-10-20).
- Means: a checkable promise for the late-Jan 2027 call. Rate-hike expectation matches the regime read (Fed leaning to hike). Confidence: medium.

**4. Efficiency: promises not delivered; explanation shifted.**
- FY2025 efficiency guide was 32-33% (Jul 2025); Q2 2025 came in at 34.1%. Source: Fool Q2 2025 (2025-07-22).
- Jan 2026: other expense to grow in line with receivables. Apr 2026: operating leverage in the back half, operational losses called one-off to Q1. Source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/21/synchrony-syf-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/ (2026-04-21).
- Oct 2026: efficiency worsened again (35.9%); operational losses have a tail through the whole year; 100+ mitigation steps said to help 2027. Source: Webull Q3 2026.
- Means: the "idiosyncratic" label did not hold; a second thing to verify in Q4. Confidence: medium-high.

**5. Capital: same target, no timeline, "not a run rate".**
- 11% CET1 target stated Apr 2025 (CET1 13.2%) and Jul 2026 (13.2%); Q3 2026 CET1 12.9%. Management would not say when it reaches 11% and said the $1.1B Q3 buyback is not a new run rate. Q1 2026 declined quarterly pacing and said CET1 is not the binding constraint; a new $6.5B authorization began Q2 2026. Sources: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/01/26/synchrony-syf-q1-2025-earnings-call-transcript/ (2025-04-22); Fool Q1 2026; https://www.webull.com/news/15265583661720576 (2026-07-21).
- Means: CET1 has bounced between 12.6% and 13.2% over four quarters, so the thesis phrase "fell 14.3% to 12.9%" overstates the recent slide (14.3% was Q3 2025). Buyback pace is a choice, not a promise, which cuts both ways for the catalyst. Confidence: medium.

**6. Dodged questions cluster on three topics.**
- Low-income consumers: asked Q1 2026, answered only by credit score; not addressed in Q2 or Q3 2026 summaries. Recession charge-off level: "do not care to comment" (Apr 2025). Credit range and reserve direction: no point estimate (Jan 2026). 2027 numbers, Q4 consensus bridge, CET1 timeline: none (Oct 2026). Amazon Pay Later: "can't get specific" (Jan 2026). Sources: transcripts above.
- Means: stress in the weakest borrowers is the main unobserved risk; management's evidence (entry rates better than 2019 in Q2 2026, better than 2018 in Q3 2026, strong non-prime payments) is about early delinquency, which is the lead measure. Two-day delinquency was flagged as under some pressure (Q2 2026). Confidence: medium.

**7. Partners: no loss mentioned in any call; coverage of renewals is strong.**
- About 97% of top-25 partner interest and fees renewed through 2028, top five 2030+ (Jan 2026); top-five expiries 2030-2035 (Jul 2025); smaller programs below return thresholds exited (Jul 2026). Sources: Fool Q4 2025; Fool Q2 2025; Webull Q2 2026.
- Means: supports durability; the real partner risk is renegotiation (partner payment-program changes cost under $50M in net revenue in 2025) rather than loss. Confidence: medium-high.

**8. Rate cap: loud in January, silent since.**
- Jan 2026: CEO said a 10% APR cap would force sharp credit cuts and hurt partners and about 400,000 small businesses. Sources: Fool Q4 2025; https://www.americanbanker.com/news/synchrony-says-retailers-would-also-be-hurt-by-10-rate-cap (2026-01-27). Q2 2026: on late fees, "nothing's been formalized"; not discussed on Q1 or Q3 summaries.
- Means: absence of discussion is not proof the proposal is dead; dd-regulatory should check status. Confidence: low-medium.

**9. Guidance delivery on credit and EPS is good (supports).**
- 2025 NCO guide 5.8-6.0% (Apr 2025) then 5.6-5.8% (Jul 2025); 2026 NCO guide moved from 5.5-6% to under 5.5%; 2026 EPS guide $9.10-9.50 (Jan), $9.25-9.50 (Jul), about $9.50 (Oct). Sources: Fool Q1 2025, Q2 2025, Q4 2025, Q1 2026; Webull Q2 and Q3 2026.
- Open risk: mid-single-digit loan growth by year-end was held in Oct, but triage shows 3% y/y in Q3, so Q4 needs acceleration (unverified).
