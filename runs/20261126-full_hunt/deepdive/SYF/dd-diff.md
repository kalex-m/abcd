## Summary for main thread
1. **The CET1 and tangible-equity recast is a presentation change, not a restatement of earnings.** Dec 2025 CET1 went 12.6% (as filed) to 13.5% (recast); Q3 2025 went 13.7% to 14.3%. The triage's "14.3% to 12.9%" compares on the new basis. Like-for-like, CET1 fell about 0.3 points a quarter since Dec.
2. **The recast makes the triage's price cushion look bigger than it is.** TBV/share rose about $3, ROTCE restated down 1.5 to 1.9 points, and the 1.61x P/TBV is about 1.77x on the old definition (my estimate).

No earnings restatement, 10-K/A or Item 4.02 found. Call transcripts unreachable.

Thesis impact: neutral

**F2. What the recast does to the thesis numbers.** Fact: Q3 2026 CET1 12.9% is on the new basis; the same Q3 2025 quarter reads 14.3% recast vs 13.7% as filed. Dec 2025 to Sep 2026 on one basis: 13.5%, 13.6%, 13.2%, 12.9%. → 3Q26 tables (2026-10-20). Means: the drop is real (buybacks outran earnings; the company's own bridge shows -3.8 points of buybacks), but the YoY gap is 1.4 points on the recast basis and about 0.8 points on the old basis (13.7% to roughly 12.9%). Not alarming. However, **P/TBV**: triage's 1.61x uses TBV $42.79 (recast). Adding back about $1.2B of net software (10-Q note: $3.4B gross, $2.2B accumulated amortisation) on 311M shares gives TBV near $38.9 and P/TBV near 1.77x at $68.73 (my calculation, rounded inputs). ROTCE on the old definition would be roughly 1.5 to 1.9 points higher than the reported 25.2% (the observed gap in each restated quarter). The triage's own-history P/TBV range (about 1.2x to 2.0x) was built from XBRL on the old basis, so it is not comparable with the current 1.61x. Confidence: high on the recast, medium on the like-for-like estimate.
