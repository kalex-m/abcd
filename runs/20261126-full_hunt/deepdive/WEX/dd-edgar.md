# WEX dd-edgar (EDGAR layer), run 20261126-full_hunt

## Summary for main thread
1. **The 2026 earnings the triage prices (8.6x on $184.32) carry a large fuel lift.** Q3 2026 fuel averaged $4.85 a gallon vs $3.38; Mobility segment profit rose 39% in Q3 and 24% over nine months. The filing puts the fuel boost to nine-month Mobility revenue at about $119M and to Mobility margin at about 3 points. Mobility is now 54% of nine-month segment profit (49% in 2025).
2. **Benefits growth has slowed.** 2025 segment profit grew 11%; nine months of 2026 grew 4.9% and Q3 grew 0.9%. Corporate Payments profit is up 11% in 2026 (it fell 17% in 2025), so that known risk has eased.
3. **Impactive, the activist with a board seat, sold about 250,500 shares in November 2026 at $181 to $184.** The debt maturity wall (Term B-2, April 2028) was refinanced to 2033 on 2026-11-24, and the covenants are unchanged (maximum leverage 4.75x vs 2.7x actual).

Thesis impact: weakens

## Findings

**F1. Fuel is inflating 2026 profit; the "price implies flat even at 2025 fuel" test needs ex-fuel earnings.**
Fact: Average U.S. fuel price was $3.60 in Q1, $4.70 in Q2 and $4.85 in Q3 2026 (2025 quarters: $3.32, $3.28, $3.38). Mobility segment adjusted operating income: Q1 $124.5M (down from $131.4M), Q2 $182.7M (up from $134.0M), Q3 $203.9M (up from $146.7M). Fuel prices raised Q3 and nine-month Mobility revenue by about $57.3M and $119.0M and lifted Mobility margin by about 3 points. The 10-K sensitivity is about $2.0M of revenue per one cent per gallon (no fuel hedges). On that arithmetic, the $1.47 gap between Q3 2026 and Q3 2025 fuel is worth roughly $290M a year of revenue (my calculation).
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910826000043/wex-20260930.htm (as of 2026-10-29); Q1 and Q2 10-Qs 0001309108-26-000017 and -000029; 10-K Item 7A https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910826000010/wex-20251231.htm (2026-02-13).
Meaning: Mobility profit in 2026 is largely a fuel result. The $119M nine-month revenue lift exceeds the whole $98.9M Mobility profit gain, so ex-fuel Mobility profit looks flat to down, helped by cost items such as credit losses (+$34.6M, +78%) and sales and marketing (+$23.7M). The market treating WEX as a fuel bet is partly right; the cheap multiple rests on fuel-boosted earnings. Confidence: high on the facts, medium on the ex-fuel inference.

**F2. Segment mix: the non-fuel segments are not carrying the growth.**
Fact: Segment adjusted operating income, 2025 (2024): Mobility $541.1M ($598.5M), Corporate Payments $213.3M ($256.2M), Benefits $341.6M ($307.0M); Benefits is 31.2% of 2025 total. Nine months 2026 (2025): Mobility $511.1M ($412.2M), Benefits $271.2M ($258.5M, +4.9%), Corporate Payments $171.1M ($153.7M, +11.3%). Benefits by quarter vs prior year: Q1 $100.2M (+15%), Q2 $83.5M (-2%), Q3 $87.5M (+1%). Benefits margin slipped from 43.6% to 43.1%. HSA yield was 4.97% in Q3 (5.13% a year earlier); average HSA custodial cash rose 11% to $5.35B. Benefits account servicing revenue fell 3% in Q3 as deposits moved to WEX Bank (shown as other revenue instead).
Source: Q3 10-Q Note 17 and MD&A (as above, 2026-10-29); 10-K Note 23 (2026-02-13).
Meaning: The thesis line "Benefits growing about 11% a year" is a 2025 number. The 2026 run-rate is mid-single-digit, with rising costs. Rising custodial cash is offset by lower yield. Corporate Payments recovered because of a network-incentive contract amendment signed in Q3 2025, not new customers. Confidence: high.

**F3. Rate sensitivity is qualitative only; no earnings table for HSA yield.**
Fact: Item 7A says HSA assets are "predominantly invested in laddered fixed rate securities" that protect revenue from the full impact of rate changes, but a sustained rate decline reduces yield; rising rates may require paying partners or account holders more (caps in most contracts). The only quantified rate item is fair value of $4.3B available-for-sale securities: a 25 bp rise cuts value under 2%. The 10-Q (Q3 2026) says no material change.
Source: 10-K Item 7A (2026-02-13); Q3 10-Q Item 3 (2026-10-29).
Meaning: No filed number shows Benefits profit under rate cuts. At about $5.3B of average custodial cash, each 100 bp of yield is about $53M of annual revenue (my arithmetic, ignoring the ladder lag). Confidence: medium.

**F4. Debt wall refinanced; covenants unchanged. Two new 8-Ks since triage.**
Fact: 8-K of 2026-11-12 (Eighth Amendment): revolver and Term A maturity extended from 2029-05-10 to 2031-11-10; revolver raised $1.6B to $1.7B; Term A upsized $787.5M to $900M; spread now 1.375% over SOFR at leverage up to 3.00x, rising to 2.00% above 4.00x. 8-K of 2026-11-25 (Ninth Amendment): about $1.2B of Term B-2 (due April 2028) refinanced into Term B-4 due 2033-11-24. The 8-K text prints the B-4 margin as SOFR+0.75% and base+1.75%; the filed credit agreement (Ex. 10.1) shows SOFR+1.75% and base+0.75%, unchanged from B-2, so this is an inversion in the 8-K text, not a repricing. The 11-25 8-K also corrects a date typo in the 11-12 8-K. Financial covenants: interest coverage at least 3.00x, leverage at most 4.75x; actual leverage 2.7x at 2026-09-30. Term B-2 stood at $1,364.0M at 2026-09-30; the 8-K says "approximately $1.2 billion", a gap of about $160M I could not explain from filings.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000114036126043231/ef20085284_8k.htm (2026-11-12); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000114036126045592/ef20086273_8k.htm (2026-11-25); Ex. 10.1 ef20086273_ex10-1.htm (contains a leftover drafting note "NTD", an unusual sign of a draft-form exhibit); Q3 10-Q liquidity section.
Meaning: The 2028 maturity cliff, the main balance-sheet worry, is gone; nearest corporate maturities are now 2031 to 2033. Leverage headroom is wide. Caveat: the 2.7x is on trailing earnings that include the fuel lift. Confidence: high.

**F5. Buybacks: the 2025 share drop was mostly one debt-funded tender; the 2026 program is real but modest so far.**
Fact: Feb-Mar 2025 modified Dutch-auction tender: about 4.9M shares at $154, $750M, funded by $550M 6.500% senior notes due 2033 plus a $450M Term B-3 (8-K 2025-03-06). Shares outstanding: 38.8M (2025-02-13), 34.3M (2026-02-06), 32.86M (Form 144 cover data, Nov 2026). The prior authorization expired 2026-01-01 (10-K risk factor); a new $1.0B authorization came 2026-05-14. Q3 2026: 1,092,821 shares at $183.01 average (July $154.86, August $193.63, September $189.21); $740.0M remained at 2026-09-30, so about $260M used since May.
Source: 10-K Note and Item 1A (2026-02-13); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000114036125007445/ny20044175x8_8k.htm (2025-03-06); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000131010826000019/ (8-K 2026-05-14, accession 0001309108-26-000019); Q3 10-Q Item 2.
Meaning: Buybacks are supportive but they are paid at prices near today's, and the 2025 tender added about $1.0B of debt. Repurchasing while fuel flatters earnings is the opposite of buying cheap on a trough. Confidence: high.

**F6. The activist is a seller.**
Fact: Impactive Capital (board designee Lauren Taylor Wolfe) held 1,707,253 shares (about 4.9%) per Schedule 13D/A of 2026-04-01. Form 4s show open-market sales: 200,000 at $181.00 on 2026-11-05; 34,577 at $184.19 on 2026-11-06; 15,929 at $183.27 on 2026-11-17 (about 250,506 shares, roughly $45.8M). Holding after the last sale: 1,456,951 (about 4.4% of 32.86M shares). The Cooperation Agreement (8-K 2026-05-04, Ex. 10.1) requires Impactive to keep at least the lesser of 3% or 1,040,044 shares to retain its replacement-director right; it also has standstill and voting commitments, and ends 30 days before the 2027 nomination deadline or 10 days after the designee leaves, whichever is later. The agreement also required the board to split the Chair and CEO roles after the annual meeting; no later 8-K Item 5.02 reports that split.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000092189526002975/ (Form 4 2026-11-09); .../000092189526003222/ (Form 4 2026-11-19); .../000092189526003211/ (Form 144 2026-11-17); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000121390026051676/ea0288872-8k_wex.htm (2026-05-04).
Meaning: The triage catalyst lists "a board now with an Impactive designee". The designee's fund is trimming near $181 to $184, which cuts against the "someone is pushing value out" story. It is still above the 3% floor. Whether the sales are under a 10b5-1 plan is not stated in what I read (dd-ownership). Confidence: high on trades, low on intent.

**F7. Risk-factor diff (FY2025 vs FY2024 10-K): few real changes.**
Fact: Summary list is 45 headings in both years. New or changed: (a) new "stockholder activism" risk naming Impactive's 2026-02-09 four-nominee letter and proxy-contest costs; (b) credit and fraud losses risk reworded (drops "substantial"); (c) ILC risk now includes more institutions being allowed to do bank-like activities; (d) FHLB advances added to the brokered-deposit funding risk; (e) debt risk now includes variable-rate debt, and the standalone interest-rate-fluctuation risk was merged into it; (f) "climate change" became "sustainability issues". The fuel risk adds "or decline" in prices and "advances in oil production technologies"; EV wording is unchanged in substance ("mixed-fleet future").
Source: 10-K FY2025 vs 10-K FY2024 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910825000017/wex-20241231.htm (2025-02-20), Item 1A.
Meaning: No new disclosed threat to the franchise. Confidence: medium (headline-level diff plus spot reading, not every paragraph).

**F8. Audit, controls and red flags are clean; pay is fuel-linked.**
Fact: Deloitte unqualified opinion; internal control effective at 2025-12-31; no material weakness. Single critical audit matter in both FY2025 and FY2024: revenue (highly automated, many low-dollar transactions). No 8-K Item 4.01 or 4.02, no NT 10-K or 10-Q since 2019, no CORRESP/UPLOAD since 2016. Legal proceedings: estimate of reasonably possible loss "not material" in FY2024, FY2025 and Q3 2026; the FY2025 wording lists more categories of dispute. 2025 proxy: annual bonus 60% adjusted operating income and 40% adjusted revenue; three-year share units 60% adjusted EPS and 40% adjusted net revenue (2022 grants paid 163.8%). Q3 10-Q says stock-comp rose because higher fuel prices lifted expected payouts. 2026 votes: say-on-pay 30.86M for vs 1.23M against; Deloitte ratified 31.27M to 0.83M.
Source: 10-K (2026-02-13), 2025 DEF 14A https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910825000073/wex-20250417.htm (2025-04-17), 8-K 2026-05-14.
Meaning: Accounting risk is low. Management pay rises with fuel-inflated metrics, so insiders are rewarded for the same fuel windfall the thesis says the market overweights. Confidence: high.
