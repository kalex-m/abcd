# WEX deep-dive focus

**Thesis under test (from triage):** the market treats WEX as a levered fuel-price bet and prices all of it at 8.6x 2026 adjusted EPS ($184.32; EV ~$9.0B). But Benefits (~31% of 2025 segment profit, growing ~11% a year, HSA custodial cash earning interest) and buybacks (diluted shares -4% in a year) do not depend on fuel; the price implies flat-to-falling owner cash flow even at 2025 fuel prices. Catalyst: Q4 2026 results and 2027 guidance (~early Feb 2027) showing growth ex-fuel, buybacks, leverage under 2.7x, board changes.

**Known risks:** fuel demand shrinking long-term (electrification); Corporate Payments lost a legacy customer and its profit is falling; net leverage ~2.7x; Mobility credit losses on small fleets; Benefits income falls if rates are cut; adjusted EPS vs GAAP gap.

**Layer tests**
- dd-edgar: segment disclosures and their changes, fuel-price and rate sensitivity tables, risk-factor diff, CAMs, 8-K Items (1.01, 2.03, 5.02 board changes), any 13D-related agreements filed as exhibits.
- dd-ir: investor-day targets vs delivery; non-GAAP reconciliation size; guidance history; fuel-price assumptions behind guidance.
- dd-transcripts: wording on Corporate Payments customer loss, Benefits growth, EV charging, buyback vs deleveraging priorities; promises vs delivery.
- dd-regulatory: bank charter (WEX Bank) regulation, any FTC/CFPB actions on fleet-card fees (Corpay precedent), state matters, legal-proceedings note year over year.
- dd-credit: full debt maturity ladder (term loans, notes, securitisation, deposits at WEX Bank), covenants and headroom (leverage covenant), interest-rate hedges, buybacks vs FCF, share count.
- dd-ownership: open-market Form 4 trades vs 10b5-1; SC 13D/13G (activists); 13F changes; short interest.
- dd-peers: Corpay (CPAY), Edenred/Fleetcor-style peers, HSA peers (HealthEquity HQY): same trends? Customers' or partners' filings naming WEX.
- dd-diff: guidance given vs actual for 8 quarters (and how much fuel-price changes explained misses); restated numbers; wording diff.
