## Summary for main thread
1. Fuel, not operations, drove 2026: of the $3.83 rise in FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance (midpoint $17.55 in Feb to $21.38 in Oct), $3.56 (93%) was labelled fuel by WEX itself. Management's own fuel sensitivity is about $0.35 EPS per $0.10 a gallon, so at 2025's $3.32 the guide would be roughly $17.3 to $17.9, not $21.38.
2. Benefits is not growing 11% now: segment profit +4.9% in the first nine months of 2026 ($271.2M vs $258.5M), share of segment profit 28.4%. But Corporate Payments profit is recovering (+11%), and WEX says rate cuts help net EPS (+$0.45 per -100 bps), which counters the cut-risk worry.
3. GAAP vs adjusted EPS gap is large: $9.20 vs $15.61 for nine months of 2026 (41%). Stock pay is the part that is a real cost: $112.4M in nine months, already above all of 2025 ($103.5M).
Thesis impact: weakens

## Findings

**F1. Guidance revisions in 2026 were nearly all fuel.** Walk of FY2026 adjusted EPS guide: $17.25-17.85 (2026-02-04, fuel assumed $3.10) → $18.95-19.55 (+$1.70, all fuel, 2026-04-22) → $19.68-20.08 (+$0.63, of which fuel $0.46, 2026-07-22) → $21.28-21.48 (+$1.50, of which fuel $1.40, assumed $4.50, 2026-10-28). Non-fuel total about $0.27 (1.5%). → Q3 supplement https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910826000039/wexq32026earningssupplement.htm (2026-10-28); earlier supplements same accession pattern (000130910826000014, 000130910826000026, 000130910826000005). → The "8.6x" is on an EPS that is about 20% fuel-lifted; at 2025 fuel it is about 10.3-10.7x (my arithmetic: $184.32 / $17.3-17.9). Still cheap, but less. Confidence: high on the walk, medium on the re-based figure.

**F2. Fuel and rate sensitivity quantified.** +$0.10 a gallon = about $20M revenue and $0.35 adjusted EPS; +100 bps rates = $30M revenue and -$0.40 EPS; -100 bps = +$0.45. WEX says it targets a "materially neutral" rate effect and uses hedging/balance-sheet choices. Same supplement (2026-10-28), page 17. → Rate cuts do not hurt net EPS on management's numbers (Benefits float income lost, but funding costs fall more). Falling fuel is the live risk: Q4 guide assumes $4.81 (NYMEX curve, 2026-10-19); each $1 a gallon below that is about $3.50 annualised EPS (about $0.9 per quarter). Confidence: medium (company approximation, drifts with balance sheet).

**F3. Guidance hit rate: 7 of 7 quarters above the top of the EPS range; ex-fuel, 2026 is in line to slightly light.** Actual vs guide (adjusted EPS): Q1'25 $3.51 vs 3.35-3.50; Q2'25 $3.95 vs 3.60-3.80; Q3'25 $4.59 vs 4.30-4.50; Q4'25 $4.11 vs 3.76-3.96; Q1'26 $4.15 vs 3.80-4.00; Q2'26 $5.35 vs 4.93-5.13; Q3'26 $6.13 vs 5.45-5.65. In Q3'26 actual fuel was $4.85 vs $3.90 assumed (~$0.8 EPS by the sensitivity /4) against a $0.58 beat; Q1'26 $3.60 vs $3.09 assumed (~$0.45) against a $0.25 beat. Sources: releases wexq32026earningsrelease.htm etc. under https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/ (accessions 000130910825000083, ...171, ...187, 000130910826000005, ...014, ...026, ...039; 2025-04-30 to 2026-10-28). → Management guides to futures at quarter start, so beats look like conservatism but 2026 beats are mostly fuel. FY2025 beat ($16.10 vs initial $14.65-15.25) was largely the $750M tender offer (diluted shares guided 39.0M, actual 35.9M). Confidence: medium (sensitivity is rough).

**F4. Investor-day targets: revenue behind, profit ahead, and fuel-helped.** 2022 Investor Day (2022-03-23): five-year targets, at stable fuel and FX, of organic revenue +8-12% a year, total revenue +10-15%, adjusted net income +15-20%. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000115752322000359/a52609898ex99_2.htm (2022-03-23). Delivery from company chart: revenue $1.9B (2021) to $2.66B (2025), about 8.8% a year; FY2026 guide ~$2.98B is about 9.4% a year over five years, below the 10-15% total target and helped by $91M+ fuel. Adjusted EPS $9.14 to $16.10 (2025), about 15% a year; $21.38 guide = about 18.5% over five years. Chart: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910826000039/wexlong-termgrowth.jpg (2026-10-28, 2021 revenue read from chart, rounded). Caveat: target was adjusted net income, I compared EPS (buybacks flatter EPS ~20% fewer shares since 2020 per company). A secondary report says a 5-10% organic target was given at a June 2025 conference; no primary source found. → Profit target met mainly via buybacks, fuel and margin; revenue target missed. Confidence: medium.

**F5. Non-GAAP gap.** FY2025 GAAP EPS $8.47 vs adjusted $16.10 (gap $7.63): acquired-intangible amortization $5.34, stock pay $2.88, other costs $0.71, impairment $0.28, offset by tax items -$2.13. Nine months 2026: GAAP $9.20 vs adjusted $15.61: amortization $3.94, stock pay $3.23, other $0.68, tax -$1.64. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000130910826000039/wexq32026earningsrelease.htm (2026-10-28); FY2025 in .../000130910826000005/q42025earningsrelease.htm (2026-02-04). → Amortization is non-cash; stock pay is a real cost and is accelerating (quarterly $29.6M, $37.4M, $45.3M in 2026 vs $13.3M-$34.7M in 2025; 9M $112.4M vs $80.4M). Cause not explained in the releases. Treating stock pay as a cost at a ~$150M run-rate is about $4.3 a share pre-tax, about $3.2 after the 25% adjusted tax rate (my arithmetic). Confidence: high on figures; reason for rise unknown.

**F6. Adjusted free cash flow versus GAAP operating cash flow.** Nine months 2026: operating cash flow -$419.6M (2025: +$159.6M); adjusted FCF $502.8M after adding $1,277.9M of deposit and borrowing funding, less $565.3M investment purchases, plus $302.0M release of WEX Bank cash. Same release, Exhibit 1. → Adjusted FCF treats deposits that fund fuel receivables as cash generated. Higher fuel prices inflate receivables; the number is a management construct and should not be capitalised like ordinary FCF. Capex $128.0M (9M). Confidence: high on figures.

**F7. Segment profit split.** Segment adjusted operating income FY2025: Mobility $541.1M, Benefits $341.6M (31.2%), Corporate Payments $213.3M; FY2024: $598.5M / $307.0M / $256.2M. Nine months 2026: Mobility $511.1M (+24%, margin 42.5%), Benefits $271.2M (+4.9%, margin 43.1% vs 43.6%), Corporate Payments $171.1M (+11%). Q3'26 Benefits revenue +4.8%, margin 42.2% (higher depreciation); custodial cash $5.3B (+11.2%) yield 4.97%. Supplement Q3'26 (above). HSA accounts +8% to 9.4M (Apr 2026 fireside chat, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1309108/000121390026048251/ea0287790-defa14a_wexinc.htm, 2026-04-28). → Benefits "31%" holds for 2025 but 2026 growth is ~5%, not 11%, and its share is 28% as fuel lifts Mobility. Weakens the "non-fuel engine" half of the thesis. Corporate Payments "profit falling" is stale: it fell in 2025 and has rebounded in 2026. Confidence: high.

**F8. Management claims in the proxy-fight material.** Fireside chat (2026-04-27) and presentation: 2026 plan assumes +75 bps margin improvement "macro neutralised"; $70M Mobility price increases 2024-25, continuing in 2026; $110M+ run-rate savings since 2022 and $50M more planned in 2026; over $2B bought back since 2020, share count down ~20%; board rejected a Benefits spin or sale after review with BofA and J.P. Morgan; EV: ~175,000 charging ports on the new fleet card, with US adoption "not as much as we would like". Source: same DEFA14A (2026-04-28). → Cost and price levers are real but self-reported. Benefits separation, one possible re-rating route, was declined. Confidence: medium (management statements).

**F9. Leverage and buybacks (from supplements).** Leverage covenant ratio 2.7x (Sep 2026) from 3.1x (Mar); about $260M repurchased through Q3 2026; FY2026 diluted share guide 34.6M. Q3 release (2026-10-28). Confirms the thesis' buyback and deleveraging points. Confidence: high.
