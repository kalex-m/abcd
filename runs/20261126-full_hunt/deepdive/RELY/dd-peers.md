# RELY dd-peers (peers, partners, primary industry data)

## Summary for main thread
1. Peers confirm a digital shift and share gain: WU Branded Digital transactions +21% but digital revenue only +8% (revenue per digital transaction down about 11%); Euronet digital C2C +34% while its cross-border profit fell 28% (9M); Intermex retail transactions -14.4%. Remitly (+25% volume, +22% revenue) is outgrowing all of them.
2. Price pressure is real and industry-wide: Wise cut take rate to 0.50% (-2bp) and Euronet revenue per transaction fell. Remitly's own take rate (revenue / send volume) slipped about 6bp (2.15% to 2.09%, derived).
3. Official Mexico and Philippines inflows grow only about 2% YTD, so Remitly's +25% is share gain, not market growth. No partner filing names Remitly; only Intermex names it as a rival.
Thesis impact: supports (take-rate risk is the watch item)

## Findings
1. **Western Union digital: volume up, revenue per transaction down.** Q3 2026 Branded Digital transactions +21%, revenue +8% (constant currency +8%); total Consumer Money Transfer revenue -2%, transactions +2%; North America revenue -7%, transactions -4%, tied to immigration policy. WU says it has historically cut prices "in response to competition". → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1365135/000119312526447662/wu-20260930.htm (as of 2026-09-30) → The incumbent's digital arm grows by pricing lower; Remitly's competitors are matching growth with margin sacrifice. Confidence high.
2. **WU says the remittance tax is in force and Treasury proposed rules in April 2026**; 1% on cash-funded sends. Same 10-Q (2026-09-30). → Supports the thesis that the tax hits cash/retail, where WU and Intermex are weak. Confidence high.
3. **WU-Intermex merger terminated.** California DFPI imposed conditions WU would not accept; termination effective 2026-11-12, no fee. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1365135/000119312526457046/d500072d8k.htm (2026-11-12) → Removes a possible bigger retail rival; Intermex stays independent and shrinking. Neutral to mildly positive. Confidence high.
4. **Euronet cross-border (Ria, Xe, Dandelion).** Q3 revenue $431.0M (-5%); 9M operating income $122.5M (-28%); Q3 operating margin 8.7% vs 13.1%; revenue per transaction $9.51 vs $9.83; digital C2C transactions +34%; filing blames the 1% remittance tax and immigration policy for lower retail volume. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1029199/000155485526002297/eeft-20260930.htm (2026-09-30) → Confirms the same split: digital strong, retail hit. Euronet's Dandelion sells payout/API access to fintechs, a possible entrant route. Confidence high.
5. **Intermex (retail rival) shrinking.** Q3 revenue $126.4M vs $154.9M; transactions -14.4%, principal -13.7%, blamed on a "contraction in the retail remittance market, particularly the LAC corridor". It lists Remitly with WU, MoneyGram and Euronet as competitors. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1683695/000162828026072217/imxi-20260930.htm (2026-09-30) → Retail loses, which is where Remitly's share comes from. Only named mention of Remitly in any peer or partner filing found. Confidence high.
6. **Wise (foreign; interim FY27 to 2026-09-30), via secondary sources only.** Cross-border volume $141.0B (+24%), US volume +30%, take rate 0.50% vs 0.52% (reinvesting operating leverage into lower prices), marketing spend +46%, FY27 revenue growth guide 17.5-20%. → https://capital.com/en-gb/news/wise-group-plc-h1-results-for-fiscal-year-2027 and https://www.fxcintel.com/research/analysis/wise-h1-2027-earnings (2026-11-12) → A well-funded rival is growing as fast as Remitly in the US and cutting price on purpose. The primary release was not reachable; treat figures as medium confidence.
7. **Mexico official data.** Banxico: Sept 2026 inflow $5,362M (+1.6%); Jan-Sep $47,164M (+2.1%); average remittance $426 vs $393; 53.6% of electronic remittances paid into accounts, 46.4% cash pick-up. → https://www.banxico.org.mx/publicaciones-y-prensa/remesas/%7B29952C3D-1F6B-1CF3-9AD6-0DC1B3912796%7D.pdf (2026-11-03) → Market is nearly flat, so Remitly's growth is share gain. Account deposits are the digital-friendly channel and are over half the flow. Confidence high.
8. **Philippines official data.** BSP cash remittances Sept 2026 $3.20B (+2.6%), Jan-Sep $26.63B (+2.3%), US 39.8% of the month. → https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/economy/1009652/cash-remittances-rose-to-3-20b-in-sept-2026-bsp/story/ (2026-11-16, reporting BSP data) → Same slow-growth picture; no collapse in US-origin flow. Confidence medium (news report of BSP data; BSP page not opened).
9. **Remitly take rate, derived.** Q3 revenue +22% ($90.3M increase, so about $419.5M prior and $509.8M now) vs send volume +25% ($24.4B, about $19.5B prior): revenue/send volume about 2.15% to 2.09%. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm (2026-09-30), analyst arithmetic → Remitly is also trading price for volume, mildly. Triage's "shrinking take rate" fear is visible but small. Confidence medium.
10. **Not found:** India (RBI) 2026 inflow data by US share (only 2023-24 US share 27.7% surfaced); World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide 2026 (site 403; latest seen global average 6.49% in Q1 2025, via search summary, not opened).
