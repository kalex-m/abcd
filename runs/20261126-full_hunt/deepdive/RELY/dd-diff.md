# RELY dd-diff (what changed between versions)

## Summary for main thread
1. The only recast is Adjusted EBITDA: from Q1 2025 the company stopped counting payroll taxes on stock pay as a cost. 2024 quarters rose by $0.7M to $3.5M each (FY2024 $134.8M to $141.2M). Revenue, send volume, customers and GAAP net income were never restated. Guidance since Feb 2025 is on the new basis, so beats are like for like.
2. Revenue beat vs guide midpoint has shrunk: +7.3% (Q2'25), +3.6%, +3.6%, +2.3%, then +0.8% in Q3'26. All 8 quarters still beat. Adjusted EBITDA beats stayed large (+30% in Q3'26).
3. Q3'26 tax rate was 3.2% after the valuation-allowance release; at 21% net income would be about $55M, not $67.6M. Q3 free cash flow grew only 7%. "Transaction margin" (the +312bp) is a metric first shown in Aug 2026.

Thesis impact: neutral (revenue beat fading is a mild negative; no restatement or accounting red flag found)

## Findings

**F1. Adjusted EBITDA recast, 2024 periods (old vs new)**
- Fact: Q1'24 $19.3M to $22.8M; Q2'24 $25.1M to $26.2M; Q3'24 $46.7M to $47.4M; Q4'24 $43.7M to $44.7M; FY2024 $134.8M to $141.2M. Cause: payroll taxes on stock-based pay (Q3'24: $0.733M) are now added back. Old values: Q1'24 release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217024000121/rely1q24-earningspr.htm (2024-05-01); Q2'24 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217024000186/rely2q24-earningspr.htm (2024-07-31); Q3'24 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217024000222/rely3q24-earningspr.htm (2024-10-30); Q4'24 and FY https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000016/rely4q24-earningspr.htm (2025-02-19). New values: Q1'25 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000092/rely1q25-earningspr.htm (2025-05-07, states the recast and that the Feb 19, 2025 guide used the new basis); Q3'24 in https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000236/rely3q25-earningspr.htm (2025-11-05); Q4'24 and FY in https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009031/rely4q25-earningspr.htm (2026-02-18). Q2'24 $26.2M is as shown in the Q2'25 release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000182/rely2q25-earningspr.htm (2025-08-06).
- Means: a definition change that lifts the base year and makes 2025 growth look smaller, not larger. It also means 2025 and 2026 EBITDA excludes about $6-7M a year more stock cost than the old basis. Payroll taxes on stock pay are real cash, so the 2026 EBITDA guide is flatter than cash. 9M'26 payroll tax add-back: $5.6M (9M'25 $6.3M).
- Confidence: high.

Sources: same releases; Q4'26 guide https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/rely3q26-earningspr.htm (2026-11-04).

The high-teens 2027 growth guide would be set against a Q4'26 exit growth of 20-21%. Confidence: high on facts, low on forecast.

**F5. Guidance wording changed on profit**
- Fact: Feb 2026 guide said "GAAP net income to be positive" for FY26 and Q1. From May 2026 it says "year over year growth in net income" (Q2'26 and Q3'26 guides likewise). Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009031/rely4q25-earningspr.htm (2026-02-18); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026031305/rely1q26-earningspr.htm (2026-05-06).
- Means: the bar moved from a plain positive number to growth over a 2025 base; after the Q2 tax release, FY26 net income will be far above 2025 ($67.9M) on the tax benefit alone, so the new wording is easy to meet. Confidence: medium.

**F6. Tax benefit and Q3'26 tax rate**
- Fact: Q2'26 net income $205.9M includes a $140.6M one-time benefit from releasing the U.S. valuation allowance. The 9M benefit line is $(136.0)M (the $136M in the brief). Q3'26 tax was $2.2M on pre-tax $69.8M (3.2% rate); 10-Q attributes the gap to the allowance release without further detail. At 21% tax Q3 net income would be about $55.1M (my arithmetic). Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm (2026-09-30); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026053361/rely2q26-earningspr.htm (2026-08-05).
- Means: "positive GAAP earnings without tax benefits" (the catalyst test) needs a clean tax line; Q3 is still partly flattered. Confidence: medium (cause of the low Q3 rate not disclosed).

**F7. New non-GAAP metric: transaction margin**
- Fact: "transaction margin" (revenue less transaction expenses, over revenue) appears in no release before Aug 2026 (0 mentions in the nine prior releases checked; 13 in Q2'26, 14 in Q3'26). Q3'26 68% vs 65% ($347.4M vs $272.8M). It is arithmetic on GAAP lines, so not a restatement. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/rely3q26-earningspr.htm (2026-11-04).
- Means: the +312bp headline is real but the metric was introduced after margins had turned up. Confidence: high.

**F8. Other non-GAAP drift**
- Fact: "Integration, restructuring, and other costs" became "Restructuring and other costs"; 9M'26 $17.9M vs $3.9M in 9M'25, added back to Adj EBITDA. Q3'26 FCF $87.8M vs $81.8M (+7%), versus 9M $290.6M vs $192.8M (+51%). Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/rely3q26-earningspr.htm (2026-11-04).
- Means: add-backs are growing (about 5% of 9M Adj EBITDA) and the FCF surge is concentrated in the first half. Confidence: high.

**F9. Risk-factor diff, 10-K FY2024 (2025-02-19) vs FY2025 (2026-02-18)**
- Added or sharpened: (a) money-transfer tax: now says taxes "have been enacted at the federal level", names the excise tax, and warns it could expand beyond cash-based transfers and beyond 1%; FY24 only said governments "could" mandate a remittance tax. (b) Immigration: FY25 adds that tax risk is tied to "sentiment towards immigration", the possibility of rules requiring verification of immigration status, and says federal action or inaction on reform may spur state taxes; FY24 had no "deport" language, FY25 has one mention. (c) New risk factor on the liquidity product (advances to customers, repayment risk); not in FY24. (d) Stablecoins moved from a passing mention to a listed competitor class, with Remitly saying it views them as a rail it may use. (e) Tariffs and the OBBBA (July 4, 2025) now named; Pillar Two minimum tax, NOL/R&D credit limits, NYDFS cybersecurity rule, AI regulation (EU AI Act, Colorado) added. (f) Concentration: India, Mexico, Philippines were 55%, 49%, 46% of revenue for 2023, 2024, 2025. (g) Quarter-end day-of-week cash timing and a JPMorgan-led revolver ("2025 Revolving Credit Facility") added.
- Removed or softened: "history of operating losses ... no assurance our business will become consistently profitable" became "In prior periods, we have experienced operating losses ... remain profitable", with 2025 net income of $67.9M cited. The long culture, DEI and Pledge 1% passages were cut (workforce text, not risk). Competition now says "cross-border payments" instead of "remittances". My comparison matched paragraphs by text similarity; 63 removed and 68 added paragraphs were mostly rewordings.
- Not found: the FY25 key-person factor still names Oppenheimer as CEO and does not mention Gunningham (0 hits), though the 10-K was filed the day the CEO change was announced. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000018/rely-20241231.htm and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009038/rely-20251231.htm.
- Means: management has been writing the remittance tax and immigration risk in more specific terms since the law passed; the Q3'26 10-Q says the OBBBA tax is not expected to be material. Confidence: medium-high.
