## Findings
**F1. Low cash taxes are a timing shield, not a permanent rate.**
Fact: FY2025 income taxes paid $2.72M (federal $0.2M) against pre-tax income $71.6M. Gross deferred tax assets $211.7M, full valuation allowance $197.5M. US federal NOLs $191.9M, federal R&D credits $46.5M, capitalised research cost asset $69.6M. Q2 2026: allowance released, $140.6M discrete benefit; deferred tax assets $149.3M at 9/30/26. The 10-Q says new law's immediate R&D expensing "reduce[s] our cash tax payments in the near term".
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009038/rely-20251231.htm (2025-12-31); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm (2026-09-30).
Meaning: my arithmetic: at a 21% federal rate, 9M26 pre-tax of $186.5M implies about $39M of tax versus single-digit millions paid. A $149M asset funds roughly 2-4 years of shield, after which owner FCF is about 15% lower than today's reported figure. The valuation must use a taxed FCF. Confidence: high on facts, medium on the size of the effect.

**F2. FCF series depends on a presentation change that excludes customer funds.**
Fact: From Q4 2025 customer-funds items (prefunding, receivable, customer liabilities) moved from operating to financing cash flow. FY2024 operating cash flow was first reported at $194.5M and is now $111.6M. FY2025 financing shows -$208.7M for customer funds, FY2024 +$85.1M, FY2023 -$120.4M, 9M26 +$71.3M. 9M26 operating cash flow $309.5M, capex $18.9M, so FCF $290.6M. Net customer-funds assets (prefunding + receivable - liabilities) were $508M at 12/31/25 and $431M at 9/30/26.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm (2026-09-30); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000018/rely-20241231.htm (2024-12-31).
Meaning: the current method is the conservative one for 9M26 (it leaves out a $71M inflow). But FY2025's jump in FCF is partly a classification effect: on the old basis FY2025 operating cash flow would be about $116M (my arithmetic) versus $194.5M for FY2024. Cumulative customer-funds absorption 2023 to 9M26 is -$172.7M, about 24% of cumulative conformed FCF ($725M). Growth consumes cash that FCF does not show. Confidence: high.
