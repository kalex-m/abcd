# RELY (Remitly Global) deep-dive focus

**Thesis under test (from triage):** the market prices Remitly as a fragile immigrant-remittance trade (deportations, a remittance tax, a shrinking take rate), yet the tax targets cash sends rather than digital, send volume is +25%, transaction margin is up 312bp, and FCF after stock pay is up sharply. At $21.68 (EV ~$4.0B, $669M cash, no debt) the price needs only ~5% owner FCF growth for 10 years vs 20%+ delivered. Catalyst: Q4 2026 results and 2027 guidance (~mid-Feb 2027) showing ~20% growth holding, positive GAAP earnings without tax benefits, buyback execution under the $500M authorisation.

**Known risks:** FCF flattered by low cash taxes; executive pay has no financial metrics; Canada revenue -3%; CEO change on 2026-02-19; low switching costs; stablecoin and neobank entrants; immigration-policy risk on its core customer; transaction (fraud) losses.

**Layer tests**
- dd-edgar: risk-factor diff (immigration, remittance tax, licensing), CAMs, 8-K Item 5.02 (CEO change terms), S-8 dilution, tax footnote (NOLs, valuation allowance, why cash taxes are low), segment/geography disclosures.
- dd-ir: investor-day targets vs delivery; non-GAAP reconciliation (stock pay); guidance history; buyback authorisation terms.
- dd-transcripts: wording on the remittance tax, enforcement, take rate, marketing efficiency, new CEO's priorities; promises vs delivery.
- dd-regulatory: the remittance excise tax statute text, effective date and exemptions (card/bank-account funded transfers), IRS/Treasury guidance, CFPB remittance rule, state money-transmitter consent orders, FinCEN matters; legal-proceedings note year over year.
- dd-credit: no debt, so focus on cash and customer-funds segregation, credit facility terms, buybacks vs FCF, stock-based pay dilution and share count trend.
- dd-ownership: open-market Form 4 trades vs 10b5-1 (founder/insider selling pace), 13D/13G, 13F changes, short interest.
- dd-peers: Western Union (WU), Euronet (EEFT, Ria/Xe), Wise (foreign): same corridor and digital trends? Partners' filings naming Remitly.
- dd-diff: guidance given vs actual for 8 quarters; restated numbers; risk-factor and wording diffs.
