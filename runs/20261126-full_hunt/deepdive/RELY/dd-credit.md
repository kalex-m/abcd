# RELY dd-credit: capital structure, customer funds, buybacks and dilution (as of 10-Q for 2026-09-30, filed 2026-11-04)

## Summary for main thread
1. "No debt, $669M cash" is true on 9/30/2026 but is a point-in-time picture. The revolver was drawn $155M at 12/31/2025 and was borrowed and repaid about $8.5B gross in 9M 2026 (about 4-day average term) to prefund payouts. Cash is also tied to regulatory liquid-asset rules, amount undisclosed. Net of customer-funds working capital, free cash is closer to ~$270M than $669M (my estimate).
2. Leverage covenant (4.50x net) is irrelevant at ~$3M debt; the facility is secured and was doubled to $1.0B on 2026-10-26, so banking-partner funding dependence is real but well covered.
3. Dilution beats buybacks: shares +8.0% in two years (198.1M to 214.0M) and +1.6% year to date despite $80M repurchased; the buyback pace fell from $42M (Q1) to $15M (Q3).

Thesis impact: weakens (modestly; balance sheet is safe, but "cash-rich" and "FCF after stock pay" are softer than stated)

## Findings
1. **Debt is zero at quarter-ends but the revolver is a working tool, not a spare.** 9M 2026 borrowings $8.473B, repayments $8.628B; $155.0M outstanding at 7.25% on 12/31/2025; none on 9/30/2026; average borrowing term ~4 days. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm (2026-09-30); 10-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009038/rely-20251231.htm (2025-12-31) → "No debt" depends on the day; peak intra-week debt is not disclosed. Interest expense $8.0M 9M 2026 vs $5.1M. → high.
2. **Cash vs customer money.** Unrestricted cash $669.1M; GAAP restricted cash only $2.1M. But customer funds assets are $644.5M (prefunding $287.4M + customer funds receivable $357.1M) against customer liabilities $213.3M and trade settlement liability $33.7M. Net customer-funds position ~$397M funded by the company's own cash. 10-Q also says some jurisdictions require holding eligible liquid assets equal to undisbursed customer balances (amount not disclosed). → same 10-Q (2026-09-30) → Free cash after that net position is ~$270M (my arithmetic, 669 minus 397); if the regulatory requirement is carved out too, less. The credit agreement itself defines "Netted Cash" to exclude cash a money transmitter must hold by law, so the lenders treat part of such cash as not free. → medium (the arithmetic is mine; receivables are collected funds in transit, so true free cash is likely between ~$270M and $669M).
3. **Reporting change helps comparability, not flatters.** In 2026 the company moved customer-funds swings from operating to financing cash flow and restated 2025 (+$49.7M to 9M 2025 operating cash). Operating cash flow 9M 2026 $309.5M is therefore clean of those swings; financing includes +$71.3M from customer funds (vs -$49.7M). → 10-Q (2026-09-30) → The headline OCF is not flattered by settlement timing; but the cash balance is (see 2). → high.
4. **FCF after stock pay.** 9M 2026: OCF $309.5M less capex ($8.5M plus $10.4M software) = $290.6M FCF; less stock comp $101.7M = ~$189M (9M 2025: $192.8M FCF, $79M after SBC; 2025 operating cash restated). Cash taxes paid only $7.3M in 9M 2026; a $140.6M valuation-allowance release created $144.4M of deferred tax (non-cash, backed out of OCF). → 10-Q (2026-09-30) → FCF growth is real, but low cash taxes are a shield that shrinks as loss carryforwards are used (size is for dd-edgar). → high on figures, medium on durability.
5. **Credit facility terms.** $550M at 9/30, raised to $1.0B (LC sub-limit $400M) by Amendment No. 2 on 2026-10-26; matures 2030-06-24; JPMorgan agent; secured by substantially all assets of borrowers and guarantors; SOFR+1.50% (base+0.50%), 0.25% unused fee; $81.5M standby LCs used, $468.5M unused at 9/30. → 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217026000084/rely-20261026.htm (2026-10-30); EX-10.1 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217026000084/remitly-2ndamendment8xkxst.htm; original agreement https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000095010325008001/dp230566_ex1001.htm (2025-06-24) → The doubling signals prefunding needs scaling with volume (disbursement prefunding $287M). No pricing grid; no step-down. → high.
6. **Covenant headroom.** Single financial covenant: Total Net Leverage Ratio (debt minus Netted Cash over Consolidated EBITDA) at most 4.50x (5.00x for four quarters after a qualified acquisition, twice at most), tested quarterly, in compliance. EBITDA adds back stock pay. Buybacks are unrestricted if pro-forma net leverage is under 3.00x (7.6(c)), plus baskets of the greater of $62M and 35% of EBITDA. → credit agreement §7.1, §7.6 (as above) → With ~$3M debt and cash exceeding debt, covenants impose no practical limit on repurchases. Even a full $1.0B draw would be roughly 2-3x my rough 2026 EBITDA estimate (op income + D&A + SBC about $311M for 9M). → high on terms, low on the estimate.
7. **Debt maturity ladder.** Revolver: 2030-06-24, zero drawn. Short-term debt $2.9M (acquired Rewire advance, matures Nov 2026 with automatic extension). No bonds, no term loans, no indentures (no EX-4 debt instruments). Operating lease liabilities $34.1M (8.3 current). Purchase commitments $133.9M at 12/31/2025, $30.4M due within a year. → 10-K (2025-12-31), 10-Q (2026-09-30) → No maturity wall. → high.
8. **Off-balance-sheet, pensions, ratings.** Filing states no material off-balance-sheet arrangements; $81.5M undrawn standby LCs; FX forward contracts notional $238.8M (vs $25.0M at 12/31/2025); no defined-benefit pension found; no public credit rating found (bank facility only). Remitly Australia regulatory inquiry open, no loss estimable. → 10-Q (2026-09-30) → Larger FX hedge book is new; keep an eye on counterparty collateral terms (not disclosed). → medium.
9. **Buybacks vs FCF.** Repurchases: 2025 $23.9M; 9M 2026 $80.0M (4.5M shares, ~$17.8 average); by quarter 2026 about $42.5M, $22.7M, $14.8M (Q3: 612k shares at ~$22.5-25). $96.1M left on the $200M plan at 9/30; board added $300M on 2026-10-28 (about $396M of room, before any Q4 purchases). Buybacks equal ~28% of 9M FCF and ~79% of SBC expense. → 10-Q Item 2 and Note 11 (2026-09-30); XBRL https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001782170.json (pulled 2026-11-27) → The authorisation is large relative to the actual pace; buying slowed as the price rose to the mid-$20s. A catalyst of "buyback execution" is unproven. → high.
10. **Share count and dilution.** Cover-page shares: 198.1M (2024-10-28), 206.2M (2025-08-04), 210.7M (2026-02-16), 214.0M (2026-11-02): +8.0% in two years, +1.6% in 2026. Basic weighted shares Q3 212.7M vs 207.2M (+2.7%); diluted 223.6M vs 217.2M (+2.9%). Net 2026 issuance ~7.9M gross shares (net +3.4M after 4.5M repurchased). 23.2M shares remain in the 2021 plan, 9.3M in the ESPP; 1.2M PSUs excluded as market conditions unmet. SBC falling in dollars ($101.7M vs $113.8M 9M) but net share count still rising. → XBRL companyfacts (above) and 10-Q → Real owner dilution ~2-3% a year even with buybacks, which the "5% FCF growth needed" framing should net out. → high.
11. **Banking-partner dependence.** Funding in relies on pay-in processors; payout relies on disbursement partners (India, Mexico, Philippines) prefunded 1-2 business days ahead; credit exposure to partners disclosed, no losses to date; JPMorgan is both lender and collateral agent. Transaction loss reserve $6.8M (provisions $66.4M in 9M, down from $70.9M). → 10-Q (2026-09-30) → Single-lender-group reliance is mitigated by the larger facility but a lender pulling back would hit prefunding first, not solvency. → medium.
