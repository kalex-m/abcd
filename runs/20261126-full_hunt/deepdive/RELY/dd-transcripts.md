## Summary for main thread
1. The remittance tax has changed role on the calls. In late 2025 management called it a "net benefit"; by Q3 2026 the CFO said it "significantly" helped customer acquisition in H1 2026 and will make 2027 comparisons tougher, and a Nov 2026 fireside summary calls it a pull-forward. 2027 is framed as high-teens growth, toward the low end, against about 22% in 2026. The catalyst "growth holding near 20%" is not what management is signalling.
2. Immigration enforcement was never addressed in any call read. The Q3 2025 CFO named immigration headwinds in the US and Canada; since then the topic was answered only with "resilient" or not raised. Take rate is repeatedly deflected as "not a great metric", while pricing language grew more aggressive.
3. Delivery against guidance is strong: initial 2026 view of "high teens" became about $1.99B revenue, and EBITDA guidance rose from $340-360M to $441-443M.

Thesis impact: weakens (core quality and delivery intact; the catalyst and "tax is irrelevant" framing are contradicted by management's own words)

## Findings

**F1. Remittance tax: from structural tailwind to a one-time pull-forward that laps.**
- Q3 2025: CEO called the law a tailwind from 2026-01-01; CFO called it "a net benefit" but "early days" (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/remitly-rely-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript, 2025-11-06).
- Q4 2025: CFO again called it a tailwind; Q1 2026 revenue guide leaned on early acquisition gains (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/20/remitly-rely-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript, 2026-02-18 call).
- Q1 2026: CEO said there is no "science behind the 1%" and that how much is already captured is "hard to tell" (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/06/remitly-rely-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/, 2026-05-06).
- Q2 2026: CFO said H1 share gains from the tax funded an extended "Skip the Line" campaign aimed at US offline senders (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/remitly-rely-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/, 2026-08-05 call).
- Q3 2026: CFO said it influenced acquisition "significantly" in H1 and sets a tough 2027 comparison (https://www.fxcintel.com/research/analysis/ct-remitly-q3-2026-earnings, 2026-11-05; https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262289130-, 2026-11-05). Nov 9 fireside summary (AI-generated, lower weight): pull-forward, campaign extended through Q4 (https://quartr.com/events/remitly-global-inc-rely-fireside-chat_ojEZWA7N, 2026-11-09).
- Means: the thesis says the tax "targets cash, not digital", so it is no threat. True, but it also means part of 2026's +25% volume is a one-off share shift that management itself says it cannot size. This is the main wording shift. Confidence: high on direction, medium on size (unquantified by management).

**F2. 2027 view is a step down, framed as early.**
- Q3 2025 initial 2026 view was "high teens", with Q4 to shape formal guidance; formal guide in Feb was 19-20%, then raised each quarter to about 22% (sources above). The same pattern now: 2027 "high-teens, toward the lower end", adjusted EBITDA margin "broadly in line" with 2026, with spending to "normalize" after an underspend in 2026 (https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262289130-, 2026-11-05).
- Means: management has under-called twice, so low-end high-teens may be conservative, but 2027 margin is not guided to expand, and 2026 margin was helped by lower-than-planned spend and a first-half transaction-loss benefit (Q2 2026 call). Thesis needs about 5-9% FCF growth, so a 17-19% revenue view still clears it; only the "near 20%" catalyst is weakened. Confidence: medium.

**F3. Immigration and enforcement: said once, then silence.**
- Q3 2025: CFO flagged "immigration headwinds" in the US and Canada weighing on new-customer acquisition (Fool Q3 2025 link above). Q4 2024: management said the business had been "really resilient" through political change and guidance excluded large regulatory changes (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/02/20/remitly-global-rely-q4-2024-earnings-call-transcri/, 2025-02-19). Q4 2025: an analyst asked about immigration; the answer was only that the customer is "very resilient" (Q4 2025 link). Q1 2026, Q2 2026, Q3 2026: not discussed in the content read.
- Means: the biggest named risk in the thesis gets no explicit management read. Canada revenue -3% in Q3 (triage) had no explanation in the Q3 summaries. Absence is not evidence either way. Confidence: medium (summariser may omit passages; Q1 and Q2 2025 unread).

**F4. Take rate: deflected, and pricing talk is getting sharper.**
- Reported take rate: 2.28% (Q4 2024), 2.15% (Q3 2025), 2.13% (Q4 2025), 2.05% (Q1 2026), 2.11% (Q2 2026), 2.09% (Q3 2026) (sources above). The CFO called it "not a great metric" in Q3 2025, Q4 2025 and Q1 2026, pointing to revenue less transaction expense and lifetime value instead.
- Wording shift: Q4 2024 said some savings are passed on in better prices; Q3 2025 admitted "price investments" to win large senders; Q2 2026 CEO said pricing is "getting sharper" and the company will be "very aggressive" for share; Q3 2026 CEO said price is "one of many levers" while customers "often reward the lowest price". An analyst question on whether scale savings fund lower prices or wider margin went unanswered (Q3 2026 summary).
- Means: confirms the "shrinking take rate" fear is real, but transaction margin dollars grew 27% (Q3 2026), which is the offset the thesis relies on. Confidence: high.

**F5. Marketing efficiency: a fixed phrase, improving ratio.**
- Every call from Q4 2024 to Q3 2026 repeats about 6x lifetime value to acquisition cost and payback under 12 months, with no definitions or cohort numbers added. Marketing share of revenue: 22.5% (Q4 2024), 20.8% (Q3 2025), 19.9% (Q4 2025), 18.2% (Q1 2026), 19.5% (Q2 2026), 19.7% (Q3 2026). Management guides marketing per active user up modestly in Q2, Q3 and Q4 2026, citing World Cup, UAE launch and brand campaigns (sources above).
- Means: the efficiency gain is flattening and turning slightly up as growth slows. Active customers growth (+17%) trails send volume (+25%); in Q3 2026 analysts asked why and management steered to send volume and "quality of QAU" instead of explaining. Confidence: medium.

**F6. New CEO: strategy continuity, faster pace, broader ambition, thin specifics.**
- Q4 2025 (day before start): "a little bit early" for views; AI a "big tailwind". Q1 2026: strategy unchanged, differs on pace; smaller teams, AI cost cuts. Q2 2026: flatter organisation, headcount below plan, balance reinvestment with buybacks. Q3 2026: moving "from a remittance company" to a "global financial services provider"; payroll entry planned for 2027; addressed pricing, stablecoins, neobanks and reliance on remittance (sources above; PYMNTS interview 2026-05-07 https://www.pymnts.com/news/cross-border-payments/2026/overcharged-underserved-remitly-ceo-sees-big-opening-cross-border-payments/).
- Dodges: asked to name one priority, he said he was avoiding the question (Q2 2026); on a previously discussed goal of over 10% of revenue from new streams by 2028 he said the company was not "going for 10%" (Q2 2026); receiver monetisation "not proven" (Q2 2026); "I'm being evasive" on investment split (Q3 2026).
- Means: more products and a wider remit raise execution risk on a core that is working; no stated cash-return framework beyond "balancing" buybacks. Founder said in Feb 2026 he has no plans to sell for the foreseeable future (Q4 2025 call); dd-ownership should check Form 4. Confidence: medium.

**F7. Promises versus delivery: mostly delivered or beaten.**
- Q4 2024 promised GAAP net income from Q3 2025; Q2 2025 already showed GAAP profit of $6.5M (per Q2 2025 search summary, https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2025-8-6-rely-stock/, not read directly). 2025 revenue guide $1.565-1.58B vs about $1.6B actual (Q4 2025 call summary). 2026 adjusted EBITDA guide path: $340-360M (Feb), $370-385M (May), $410-415M (Aug), $441-443M (Nov) (sources above). Q3 2025 flex-credit question: CEO would not tie the 10% non-current to losses and deferred to Investor Day.
- Means: guidance is set low and beaten, which supports management credibility. But the 2026 GAAP profit includes a $136-141M tax benefit, so "positive GAAP earnings without tax benefits" (the catalyst) remains unproven here. Confidence: high.
