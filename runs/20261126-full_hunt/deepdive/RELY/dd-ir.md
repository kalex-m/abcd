# RELY dd-ir: company disclosure layer (as of 2026-11-27)

## Summary for main thread
1. Targets set in public are being beaten early: the Dec 2025 investor day gave 2028 Adjusted EBITDA of $575-600M (20-22% margin) and revenue of $2.6-3.0B; the FY2026 guide is now $441-443M EBITDA (about 22% margin) on $1.99B revenue. FY2026 EBITDA guidance has gone from $300-320M (Dec 2025) to $442M. Eight of eight guided quarters beat on both revenue and EBITDA.
2. Stock pay is the main gap: SBC was 57% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA but 30% in 9M 2026 (dollars down 11%). Buybacks are slowing ($44M, $21M, $14M per quarter in 2026) and do not offset about $35-40M a quarter of SBC. Restructuring add-backs rose to $17.9M.
3. Gross take rate fell from 2.32% (Q3 2024) to 2.09%, but revenue less transaction expenses per dollar sent held at about 1.42%.

Thesis impact: supports

## Findings
1. **Medium-term targets already ahead of path.** 2028: revenue $2.6-3.0B, Adjusted EBITDA $575-600M (20-22%), "Rule of 40" (3-year revenue CAGR plus margin at least 40%), new products 5-10% of revenue at about 30% incremental margin. FY2026 guide: revenue $1.989-1.991B (+22%), EBITDA $441-443M, about 22% margin; Rule of 40 sum is about 44% already. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009031/final4q25earningsdeck.htm (2026-02-18); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/rely3q26-earningspr.htm (2026-11-04); investor-day date and headline numbers via https://www.financialcontent.com/article/gnwcq-2025-12-9-remitly-unveils-medium-term-outlook-setting-course-for-durable-profitable-growth-at-investor-day (2025-12-09) → The EBITDA target is nearly met two years early, so either it was cautious or the extra margin is reinvested; the revenue range implies only 14-23% CAGR from 2026, so the bottom end is a deceleration. Whether management lifts the target is the Feb 2027 test. → high.
2. **Guidance history, 8 guided quarters (midpoint vs actual, $M).** Revenue: Q4-24 340 vs 351.9; Q1-25 346.5 vs 361.6; Q2-25 384 vs 411.9; Q3-25 412 vs 419.5; Q4-25 427 vs 442.2; Q1-26 437 vs 452.8; Q2-26 484 vs 495.2; Q3-26 506 vs 509.8. EBITDA: 19 vs 43.7; 38 vs 58.4; 46 vs 64.0; 54 vs 61.2; 51 vs 88.6; 83 vs 101.6; 87 vs 114.7; 93 vs 120.8. → Source: the prior quarter's EX-99.1 outlook against the next quarter's actuals, e.g. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026053361/rely2q26-earningspr.htm and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/rely3q26-earningspr.htm (2026-11-04) → 8 of 8 beats. The revenue beat has shrunk to +2.3% and +0.8% in the last two quarters; EBITDA beats stay 20-30% (cost-driven). Q4-26 guide: revenue $531-533M, EBITDA $104-106M, below Q3's $120.8M with no stated reason; guide-implied FY is conservative if the pattern holds. → high.
3. **FY2026 guide path.** EBITDA: $300-320M (Dec 2025 preliminary) → $340-360M (Feb) → $370-385M (May) → $410-415M (Aug) → $441-443M (Nov). Revenue: high-teens → $1.94-1.96B → $1.96-1.975B → $1.978-1.988B → $1.989-1.991B. → same EX-99.1 releases (2026-02-18, 05-06, 08-05, 11-04), e.g. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026031305/rely1q26-earningspr.htm → Raises came from cost, not top line (revenue guide up about 1.6% since Feb, EBITDA up about 25%). → high.
4. **Stock pay versus adjusted figures.** FY2025: Adjusted EBITDA $272.2M; SBC $155.1M (57%), SBC payroll tax $7.1M; GAAP operating income $77.5M. 9M 2026: Adjusted EBITDA $337.1M; SBC $101.7M (30%), payroll tax $5.6M, restructuring and other $17.9M (9M25 $3.9M), D&A $19.2M; GAAP operating income $189.8M. Q3-26: EBITDA $120.8M, SBC $39.6M (33%), operating income $69.4M. EBITDA less SBC and payroll tax: FY2025 about $110M; 9M26 about $230M. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/rely3q26-earningspr.htm (2026-11-04); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026009031/rely4q25-earningspr.htm (2026-02-18) → SBC dollars fell 11% year over year (9M $113.8M to $101.7M) while EBITDA rose 84%, so the stock-pay gap is closing, supporting the "FCF after stock pay" framing. "Restructuring" is no longer small. → high.
5. **Net income is flattered by tax.** 9M26 net income $322.5M includes a $136.0M tax benefit (valuation-allowance release); Q2-26 had a $140.6M discrete benefit; Q3-26 tax was $2.2M on about $69.8M pre-tax (3%). → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm (2026-11-04); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026053361/rely2q26-earningspr.htm (2026-08-05) → The guide wording moved from "GAAP net income positive" (Feb 2026) to "year over year growth in net income" (May onward); with the Q2 benefit that bar cannot be missed. Book tax should rise toward normal once the allowance is gone; GAAP EPS growth in 2027 is a weaker test than pre-tax income. Matches the triage catalyst of "positive GAAP earnings without tax benefits". → medium (future tax rate not disclosed in sources read).
6. **Take rate: gross down, net steady.** Revenue / send volume (computed): 2.32% (Q3-24), 2.23% (Q1-25), 2.15% (Q3-25), 2.05% (Q1-26), 2.09% (Q3-26). Revenue less transaction expenses / send volume: 1.52%, 1.48%, 1.40%, 1.39%, 1.42%. Transaction expenses fell to 31.9% of revenue (35.0% a year ago). Company says "lowest cost to serve wins" on pricing and cites lower transaction losses as a share of volume (no figure given; provision for transaction losses fell $3.7M over 9M). → computed from EX-99.1 releases above; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070130/remitlyirpresentation3q2.htm (2026-11-04); 10-Q https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm → Falling gross price is passed to customers and funded by cheaper network costs and lower fraud losses; net economics per dollar are stable. No transaction-loss rate is disclosed, so the fraud claim cannot be checked as a number. → medium-high.
7. **Buyback execution is thin so far.** Program $200M (Aug 2025), raised to $500M on 2026-10-28 (no expiry, no obligation). $176.1M remained at 12/31/25. Purchases: Q1-26 2.77M shares at about $16 ($44M), Q2-26 1.11M shares at $16.45-21.73 ($21M), Q3-26 0.61M shares at $22.48-25.03 ($14M); $96.1M left of the old $200M at 9/30/26, so about $396M available after the raise. → 10-Qs https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm, .../000162828026053424/rely-20260630.htm, .../000162828026031371/rely-20260331.htm (as of 2026-09-30) → The company bought hardest near $16 and slowed as price rose; $14M a quarter is under half of quarterly SBC. A larger authorisation is intent, not yet action; Q4 and Q1 repurchase tables show whether the shares at $21-22 get bought. → high.
8. **Non-GAAP definition change.** From Feb 2025 payroll tax on SBC is excluded from Adjusted EBITDA; FY2024 recast from $134.8M to $141.2M (+4.8%); Q4-24 $43.7M to $44.7M. → https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000178217025000092/rely1q25-earningspr.htm (2025-05-07); .../000162828026009031/rely4q25-earningspr.htm → The change flatters EBITDA growth by a small amount; the FY2025 +93% comparison uses the recast base. → high.
